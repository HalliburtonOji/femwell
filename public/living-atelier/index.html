What changes across the page

An engraved celestial canopy with a live Moon. Full header language in a clear opening. Open reading, a compact lesson folio, precise tide rings and dated notes. Small buds and fallen petal fragments belong to selected pauses, away from the words.

Five different worlds

01 · Celestial Marginalia — working study

Copperplate sky meets painted plant tissue. The page moves between an open reading, a small learning folio, an instrument and a diary. Fine arcs and botanical fragments carry the same drawing language through the page.

02 · Petal Architecture — concept

Enlarged translucent petal pleats form the canopy itself. Text sits in a broad clear aperture. The body uses large quiet intervals, close botanical details and folded contours, rather than a flower placed beside a title.

03 · Living Volvelle — concept

A tactile lunar instrument with an incomplete brass disc, a narrow vine and deliberately placed buds. Measured captions and code-rendered rings lead the layout; prose remains open. No baked astronomical claims in the artwork.

04 · Light Impressions — concept

Soft botanical afterimages, a sharply drawn bud and very clear dark lettering. Cool traces pass into the learning and diary areas. A contemporary white clearing, with no sepia page or floral picture frame.

05 · Nouveau Page — concept

One asymmetric organic line shapes the title clearing, then becomes a small bud at a useful junction. Different silhouettes for reading, learning and instruments. Flora directs the structure instead of decorating identical cards.
