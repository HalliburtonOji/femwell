Why the gate exists

ONS content practice includes peer and stakeholder review before publication. GOV.UK interface writing supports short, clear action labels. The current LifestyleItems schema does not allow Money, Work or Fiction as categories, or NEWS as a content type. A mapping or schema decision must be explicit before a write.
