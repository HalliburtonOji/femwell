Why this is needed

Our current source selection overweights engagement and fresh rows. In a read-only sample of the top 80 published videos, none had a stored duration and only nine had a substantial summary. A long description cannot tell us whether a video is a 20-second clip. A separate top-60 engagement sample contained 45 videos and only 15 items with a body of at least 500 characters. These are sample findings, not a verdict on every item.

First working change

  • Read: a readable source leads the calm preview, with one other read and one story visible. More exposes the other cards in the existing candidate set.
  • Listen: an active episode leads; one other listen and one watch appear first, with the other selected cards behind More.
  • Watch: unknown-length clips no longer fill the calm preview's recommended slot. The full watch directory remains available. Two existing hand-picked videos whose creator titles state 20 and 10 minutes fill a sparse slot provisionally; actual runtime/playback still needs verification.
