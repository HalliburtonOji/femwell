What goes into the Bible

Human, informed, lightly mythical. One useful thought first. Teach unfamiliar ideas briefly. Use humour occasionally; keep distress and serious decisions free of jokes. Put poetry in the supporting line and clear verbs on buttons.

Facts, folklore and reflection must be distinguishable. Full readings keep their depth. Empty history stays empty.
