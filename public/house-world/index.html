The house that grows with you.

A home you build. Jess and a pet you choose live in it. Your garden grows outside; the real sky changes around it. The world holds your life without making you play a game before you can read, plan or listen.

Step inside the touchable preview →

The small, illustrated Ideas test has pet and room choices stored on this device. This remains the research for a fuller house, not a persistent builder or a main-page change. The existing named garden bloom remains its own character.

What this could feel like

One ordinary return home

Late afternoon light crosses the room you arranged. Your chosen pet has claimed the chair you put by the window. Jess is beside the desk, not waving for attention. On it is the book you really saved, open at your real place. Outside: your garden, the current Moon, a sky that slowly shifts.

Tap the book and the full reader opens immediately. Tap Jess and she can help with the actual plan on the desk. Choose Build and you can move the chair, change the room, add a window or take it all back. Nothing in the house needs feeding because you missed a week.

What would be new

Build

Your own architecture

Begin with a beautiful furnished house. Place, rotate, undo, change materials and garden paths. Later: rooms, walls and windows. A real builder, not a wallpaper picker.

Cast

Jess, pet, bloom

Jess helps and speaks when invited. You choose and name your pet. The already named bloom keeps its garden story. They react to each other without performing for you all day.

Outside

Garden and sky

Existing plants and chapters become land beside the house. Daylight and lunar phase change the scene. Weather would need separate permission, a trustworthy source and a cost check.

Useful

Objects open real things

The book opens your exact reading place; the desk your Planner item; the player a verified Listen source. A direct route is always available. The house never becomes a navigation puzzle.

Three visual worlds to compare

MiniatureA dimensional, rotatable cutaway house: plaster, brass, dark wood, stained glass, real botanical detail and restrained character motion. My recommended first test.
GlasshouseConnected translucent conservatory rooms with living shadows and a dramatic garden/sky. Dreamlike, with a higher rendering risk.
Living folioA paper-and-ink house unfolds into depth, preserving FemWell's magazine craft. Beautiful, though it must prove it feels inhabitable.

Each would show the same Jess–pet–saved-book–moon moment. These are art directions, not finished visuals.

What the research actually says

Specific mechanics worth borrowing

The Sims separates building from living; Unpacking lets possessions tell a life story without scores; Tiny Glade makes construction forgiving and attractive; Spiritfarer makes built spaces and character relationships matter together.

Where pet and wellness apps stop short

Finch connects small actions, a pet and a home; Pocket Love combines home, pet and memory album; Kinder World brings procedural plants and gentle practice. FemWell's chance is to connect this kind of world to full real reading, planning, audio, garden and community sources. Kinder World's own farewell says it reached over 2.5 million people but ceased active development for financial reasons: visual ambition needs a sustainable production plan.

What must be proved before a pivot

The repo already has Jess memory/settings, garden companion/chapters, Sky calculations and Three.js, but no saved house/pet model. A browser 3D scene still needs an iPhone performance test: React Three Fiber documents on-demand rendering and adaptive quality; MDN warns small WebGL downloads can still cost CPU/GPU. New layout persistence, public visiting, voice and weather remain separate decisions. See ICO privacy-by-design guidance for the data boundary.

The first thing to touch

Three genuinely different visual treatments, then one Ideas slice: choose a pet, change one furnished room, watch Jess and pet react, see the garden/current Moon through the window, open a real saved book and return. Test 360/390/430 and a real iPhone, including low-motion and direct navigation. Only then decide whether the house becomes FemWell's front door or an optional world.
