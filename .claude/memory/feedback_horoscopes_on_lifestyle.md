Horoscope / zodiac content lives exclusively on the FemWell Lifestyle page. Do not put horoscope cards, "new this week" zodiac shelves, or per-sign content on the Explore page.

**Why:** Halliburton said "remove the horoscope bit we have something similar in lifestyle plus theres actually no content in the one in explore." Two problems with what I did: (1) I added horoscopes to Explore when Lifestyle already handles them — duplication, (2) the live Explore page has no horoscope content at all, so adding them is fiction not design.

**How to apply:**
- When designing Explore, skip any horoscope / zodiac / DailyStory-per-sign shelves entirely.
- If a page already has a certain content type on another tab (e.g. horoscopes on Lifestyle, Panic Mode on Today), do not replicate it elsewhere — tabs have distinct jobs.
- Verify against the live app: if the live version of a page doesn't contain a given content type, don't invent it in the demo.
