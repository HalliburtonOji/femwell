5 · Tap a small card → it covers the card face the new interaction

The mechanic: tap a small card → a panel slides up and covers the WHOLE StackedCard face (not the screen) → it holds real recommendations + todos for that thing → a small back arrow, top-left, returns to the small-card list. The board never navigates; the clipboard never moves.

Why in-board, not full-screen: we already have the full-screen ExpandDetailCard for consuming one item. This is for choosing — she stays in the board, so the back button is a real return, not a route pop. Breeze × StackedCard, per §6.7.7.

Build: a new FaceOverlay in the shared card language — position:absolute; inset:0 inside the board frame, transform: translateY(101%) → 0, ~.32s cubic-bezier(.32,.72,.24,1), reduced-motion → instant. Back arrow + Escape + tap-scrim. Contents: a hook line, 2–4 real recommendations (from the ranked feed), and 1–3 todos that write for real (Planner/Journal) and tick in place — §6.7.6, no navigation.

The hard rule on "women in the UK are saying…"

That line is a claim about real people. Three ways to source it, and only two are allowed:

  • no Invented. Never. It's a fabricated testimonial — the worst thing we could put on a wellness app.
  • no Quoting Community posts. CommunityPost/Comment exist, so it's technically easy — but those women posted anonymously into a screened support room. They did not consent to being quoted on a content card in another part of the app. That's a consent problem, not a copy problem, and I won't build it without Halli's explicit call.
  • yes Real aggregates. "38 women saved this" / "read most on Sunday mornings" — from engagement_score + LifestyleInteractions, which we already write (piece 4). No quoting, no consent issue, and it's true. Show only above a floor (say ≥10) so it's never "2 women saved this".
  • yes Real citations. Named research, the way the science footer already does it.

Recommendation: ship aggregates + citations now; take the Community-quote question to Halli separately as a consent decision (it could be done properly with an opt-in "share this line" — that's a feature, not a copy tweak).
