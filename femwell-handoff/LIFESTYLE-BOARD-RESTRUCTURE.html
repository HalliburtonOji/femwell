5 · Tap a small card → it covers the card face the new interaction

The mechanic: tap a small card → a panel slides up and covers the WHOLE StackedCard face (not the screen) → it holds real recommendations + todos for that thing → a small back arrow, top-left, returns to the small-card list. The board never navigates; the clipboard never moves.

Why in-board, not full-screen: we already have the full-screen ExpandDetailCard for consuming one item. This is for choosing — she stays in the board, so the back button is a real return, not a route pop. Breeze × StackedCard, per §6.7.7.

Build: a new FaceOverlay in the shared card language — position:absolute; inset:0 inside the board frame, transform: translateY(101%) → 0, ~.32s cubic-bezier(.32,.72,.24,1), reduced-motion → instant. Back arrow + Escape + tap-scrim. Contents: a hook line, 2–4 real recommendations (from the ranked feed), and 1–3 todos that write for real (Planner/Journal) and tick in place — §6.7.6, no navigation.

7 · Opening a card must SAY MORE the real bug

Today, opening a card echoes it. Not a theory — the code does this:

  • The collapsed card calls summaryOf(item), which returns the first substantial text it finds — for a real article that's the lede's opening paragraph.
  • The open calls paragraphsOf(item), which returns the same body, starting with that same paragraph.
  • The only de-dupe (expandCards.jsx:738) filters item.summary — not the hook the collapsed card actually showed.

So she taps expecting the piece and gets the sentence she just read. That's the bug. The fix is editorial, not decorative.

The rule: the summary is the teaser, the open is the article. Opening must reveal more AND different — never a duplicate of the collapsed text.

CollapsedOpened
Jobmake her want itgive her the thing
Showsa short enticing hook — a standfirst-length teaser, a few linesthe fuller story, properly formatted — why it matters, the context, the takeaway
First words she readsthe hooksomething new — the open leads on why_it_matters, so the top of the open is never the bottom of the card

Where the extra depth comes from — the item's own real body. Nothing is written by us, nothing is invented.

In the openReal field, already on the row
Standfirst — the line that sells the piecewhy_it_matters
The full read — every paragraph, in a proper reading columnlede / body — our articles average 4,529 chars of real writing that the card currently never shows
Pull-quote — re-hooks the scanner mid-reada real sentence selected from the body (longest clean sentence in the first third) — extracted, never authored
The takeaway — what to actually do with ittakeaways[]
Read-time — the "I can afford this" signalreadTimeLabel() — already exists

All four of those fields exist on LifestyleItems today. The depth is already in the database — the card just isn't showing it. The magazine voice is in how it's set (standfirst, reading column, pull-quote, numbered takeaways — the same devices LifestyleDetail already uses), not in new prose.

The guard: openAddsMore(item)

Before a card offers to open, check the open would carry substantially more text than the hook. If it wouldn't, the item genuinely has no fuller body — and then:

  • yes the card doesn't pretend — no expand affordance, no tap that leads to an echo.
  • yes it's reported as a content gap with a count, so we know what needs writing.
  • no we never invent the missing depth to fill the space.

The gap I already know about: the 284 videos carry ~78 chars of description — no body at all. They can't be opened up until they have one. That's exactly what the video summariser backfill is for, and it's parked on Halli's cost/risk decision — this item doesn't move it.
