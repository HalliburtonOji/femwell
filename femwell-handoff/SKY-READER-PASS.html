Your sky — the reader, mounted at last

Research per sub-piece, then the build. The full horoscope reader and the birth-chart onboarding existed, worked, and were unreachable. Now they're live from Story & sky. Plus the one thing I will not build without your sign-off. 2026-07-17.

The headline

Almost nothing needed building — it needed mounting. 15 sections, 7 hooks and a complete birth-data onboarding sheet were already written and working. They were dark, so: (a) no new user could set up a chart at all, and (b) readings stopped being generated on 2026-06-20 — because the generator is fired by the client when this tab opens, and nothing had opened it since.

The one real problem I found: moon sign, rising sign and Mercury are estimated by a language model, not computed from an ephemeris. That is flagged below, not built on.

Sub-piece 1 · Birth-chart onboarding shipped

Research: BirthDataSheet.jsx (18KB) already exists and already has the shape you asked for — date required, time optional ("optional — unlocks moon + rising"), place via OpenStreetMap Nominatim (free, no key). It writes AstroProfile {birth_date, birth_time|null, birth_place|null}.

Privacy — already correct, and worth stating: birth place is not current location and is never used as such; it's a one-off free-text city that stays optional; time is skippable; sun sign works from the date alone. Stored minimally (three fields, per-user). No tracking, no geolocation API, no coordinates.

What I added: nothing to the sheet — it was fine. The fix was reachability: it only ever opened from inside the horoscope tab, which wasn't mounted. It now opens from Story & sky. The onboarding dead-end is closed.

Sub-piece 2 · The reading — where it comes from flag

Research: generateHoroscopeReading (438 lines, deployed) writes one HoroscopeReading per user per day: a warm LLM narrative (its system prompt is genuinely good — "warm, literary, present-tense, UK English… no emoji… never invent statistics… no melodrama"), plus power/pressure/trouble cards and a cycle↔moon tie.

Is it run-dry like the Daily Story? No — and this matters. Rows run 2026-05-12 → 2026-06-20, none since, none for today. But it isn't a series that ended: the function is fired by the client on tab open (useBirthChart), and the tab has been dark since ~20 June. Nothing had asked it for a reading. Mounting the reader revives the supply — no seeding, no authoring, no new function.

Dependency to know: it needs OPENAI_API_KEY in the function env; if that ever lapses the narrative won't write. The sky itself does not depend on it (see sub-piece 3), so the surface degrades honestly instead of going blank.

Sub-piece 3 · Your sky / the almanac real, free

Research: src/utils/astrology.js already computes moon phase client-side — a synodic formula anchored to a known new moon (2000-01-06 18:14 UTC), accurate to ~±1% illumination. No backend, no key, no cost. MoonPhaseGlyph.jsx draws it as pure SVG (eight phases, no emoji codepoints). getSunSign(date) is exact date arithmetic.

What I added: the Story & sky card now shows the real moon, computed live — "The moon is waning gibbous · 62% lit" — instead of echoing whatever the last stored reading said. So the card is true today even with no reading at all, and a woman with no birth details still gets a real sky plus a warm invitation. The moon never lies and never costs anything.

The flag — the natal chart is guessed, not computed needs your call

What I found in generateHoroscopeReading: its chart step sends the birth details to an LLM with the system prompt "You estimate the moon sign, rising sign, and Mercury sign at a person's birth from their date, time and place." That is not an ephemeris. Rising changes roughly every two hours and moon sign every ~2.5 days — a language model cannot compute those; it produces a plausible sign. Planets elsewhere use "APPROXIMATE — VSOP87-equivalent mean-longitude estimates", which drift.

The evidence, from live data: one real AstroProfile reads sun=Gemini moon=Pisces rising=Aquarius from birth_place: "United Kingdom, United Kingdom" — a place string that isn't a place. A precise-sounding rising sign was returned from unusable input. Meanwhile the UI says "Birth time unlocks your rising. We need the minute" — precision language for a derivation that isn't precise.

In fairness: the app is already honest at the edges — ScienceFooter says plainly "astrology beyond moon-phase remains symbolic" and cites Helfrich-Förster 2021 / Cajochen 2013 for the moon claim, and moon/rising stay locked without a birth time. So this is not a lie; it's fabricated precision inside a symbolic frame.

My recommendation — tasteful and real: keep sun sign + moon phase as the spine (both genuinely computed, both free) and the warm reading on top. Then pick one for moon/rising: (a) relabel them honestly as estimated and drop the "we need the minute" precision language — no backend, small copy change; or (b) compute them properly with a real ephemeris library (e.g. an astronomy/ephemeris package) — that is a new dependency and almost certainly a function change, so it needs your sign-off and I have not built it. What I won't do is build more on top of the guess.

Also flagged (not built)

Components #3/#4 put every reading surface on the shared column; HoroscopeTab predates that and is a 15-section dashboard rather than long-form prose. Its narrative is short, so the mismatch is mild — but the daily narrative should move onto the column in a follow-up.   • Data hygiene: Nominatim let "United Kingdom, United Kingdom" through as a birth place. Worth a light validation if we keep place at all.
