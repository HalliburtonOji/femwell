D · THE TWO THINGS THAT NEED A DECISION

D-1 · Revive ?tab= — one small fix, many links restored

Teach the live shell to read ?tab=/?section= and focus that section. That single change makes Today's links, DailyStoryReel's three links, Jess's book suggestions and every future cross-link land correctly — instead of dumping her on the Lifestyle landing. Recommend: do it. It's additive and reversible.

D-2 · Planner can't point back at a book

PlannerItems has no source/item_id/content_type field, so a reading block is just a title string. Two options: (a) encode the reference in notes — ugly but zero schema change, ships now; (b) add two optional fields (source, ref) — clean, and it unlocks every other content type scheduling itself, but it's a schema delta needing your sign-off. Recommend (a) now, (b) proposed properly — the calendar-sync plan already asks for the same fields.

D-3 · The orphaned weekly curator — your call

weeklyBookCurator runs and writes WeeklyBookPick every week, and nothing has ever read it. Its books are commercial (ISBNs) — they can't be read in-app, so surfacing them turns the reading corner into a shop, which is off-brand. Recommend: park it (stop paying for it) or repoint the curator at readable public-domain picks so it feeds the month's set instead of my authored constant. I won't touch it without your word.

D-4 · Books lives on the demo until you flip live

None of this is reachable from /Lifestyle until the one-line clean flip lands. The wiring can be built and verified on the demo first — but it only becomes real on that flip.
