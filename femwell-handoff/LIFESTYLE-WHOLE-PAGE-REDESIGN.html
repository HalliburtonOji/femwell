FemWell · Lifestyle · whole-page redesign · report before build

One page. One language. One direction.

The Lifestyle page has been built piece by piece on local context, and it now fights itself: three header moments stacked, two visual languages on one screen, and a horoscope that is a stack of “read this” with no direction. This is the whole-page synthesis and the design to fix it as ONE thing — reported before a line is built.

STEP 2 · THE DESIGNThe whole page, as one thing

Principles that govern every band, so nothing is decided locally again: one header · one language (§2.7 clean/classy, migrated together) · one summary per view · the flora is present but placed once (the still, then meaning-rosettes and one framed feature card — never a second hero) · every control does something real · the page reads top to bottom as a directed sequence (§6.8.2 bands, §19 spine).

The page, band by band (§6.8.2 skeleton, in the clean language)

Jump-to pill + calendar icon, restyled clean (white pill, hairline, ink). Page ground = alabaster, no grain, no vignette.

ONE header — the section still

The photo/video hero and the separate script-title block are removed entirely. Drop-in contract unchanged: when a real still arrives, the placeholder is replaced by config alone.

The controller

Five chips (Read · Listen · Books · Sky · Good life) as clean white chips with a hairline; the active chip carries an ink border + its colourway meaning-rosette. Under them the state line (phase · day, or the gentle default). On the landing the active section's ONE action button shows (clean outlined). In focus, the chip is the door and the section surface owns its own CTA — no second button.

Same for the two action pills (§6.7.0a plum/gold, rendered flat and clean): landing only.

Focus: the section's bespoke §19 surface (Read · Listen · Books + story · Sky · Good life · Yours), all six migrated to the clean language together with the meaning-rosette per eyebrow kept.

Pollinator + one Cormorant-italic line; the footer band loses its paper texture and sits on the alabaster ground with the heart signature.

Books & story — smart to her, not the calendar (rendered mock: BOOKS-STORY-EXEMPLAR.html)

The miss, named honestly. The merge's rotation proof passed, but the rendered list was broken: loadStoryChapters() loads every active series and the surface listed them all, sorted by day number — two 30-chapter series interleave as 1,1,2,2… → “Chapter 9 of 60, Chapter 9 of 60”. The gate picked one series; the list never scoped to it. And even scoped, a 30-row strip on cream texture is a wall, not a design.

  • Model — unlock by calendar, position by her. Chapter N still unlocks on day N of the month's run. Her reading position = the first unread chapter (read-marks, device + user keyed). Reopening never jumps ahead; it lands on her next chapter. Behind → “3 waiting for you”; the flip control moves one at a time, forward only once the current chapter is read (‹ re-reads a finished one). Caught up → “Chapter 22 opens tomorrow.” Run closed → the finale + “a new run begins on the 1st.” Never auto-advanced.
  • Surface order. her summary → YOUR NEXT CHAPTER (the hero: title, chapter n of 30, waiting pill, drop-cap taster, Read + ‹ ›) → THE RUN (a 3×10 strip of the current series only: read / next / open / unlocks-daily; tap any open dot; at most two “up next” rows; one quiet straight-through link) → THIS MONTH'S SHELF (four books: one featured with a hook + minutes-per-chapter, two compact, the month's last book revealed on a Sunday — paced surfacing) → “Yours & the whole library ›” (a sheet: her additions + the free library, off the page).
  • Coherence of counts. A run = the current series' chapter count (30 for The Long Room); the strip, the label and the summary all read from one number. If a second series is active, it is the next run, not extra rows.
  • Wired. Read / ‹ / › → the immersive reader at that chapter; mark-as-read → read-mark + Garden; run dots → reader; featured book → in-place reading pane; reveal → nothing until its day (an honest “arrives Sunday”); library link → the sheet.

Mechanism — reversible, no new entity/function

  • One flag. LifestyleEliteShell gains a clean prop (default OFF). The demo route /LifestyleBespokeDemo passes enableFocus layout="bespoke" clean. Live /Lifestyle passes nothing → byte-identical until Halli approves; approval = one line in LifestyleElite.jsx.
  • One theme, whole tree. The shell wrapper gets .fw-clean; a scoped stylesheet (CLEAN_CSS, extended) restyles the shared primitives through class hooks added to FwCard / Clipboard / StackedCard / the glance panels (additive classNames; they render identically outside .fw-clean). Per §17.1 opt-in-off.
  • Header. SectionHeader becomes the only header when clean; placeholder stills are rendered compositions from the real engine; the drop-in contract for real stills is unchanged.
  • Horoscope. SkyFocus re-authored as the eight movements; batches 2–4 reuse the original sections' data/logic (hooks, entities, functions) and rebuild the presentation (§19.6). No new entity/function.
  • Bible. §6.8 (Lifestyle header = the section still), §6.8.2 (clean variant of the bands), §19 (the Sky as the reference composition) updated in the same cycle.
