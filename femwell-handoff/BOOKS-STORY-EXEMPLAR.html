The Books & story surface — clean language, one header

  • Smart to her. Chapters unlock one per day of the month's run (the calendar); her position is the first unread (her own, device + user keyed). Reopening never jumps ahead — it lands on her next chapter. When she's behind: “3 waiting for you”, and the flip control moves one at a time, forward only once the current one is read (‹ re-reads). Caught up → “Chapter 22 opens tomorrow.” Run closed → the finale + “a new run begins on the 1st.”
  • No wall. The run is a 3×10 strip of the current series only (the duplicate bug was two series interleaving — now the list is scoped to the run's series, so it's 30 of 30, never “of 60”). Read / next / open / unlocks-daily states; tap any open dot to jump; at most two “up next” rows; one quiet straight-through link.
  • Few, curated books. Four per month — one featured (with a hook and a ~minutes-per-chapter line, starts reading in place), two compact, and the month's last book revealed on a Sunday (paced surfacing, “tiny reveals”). Continue-reading, when it exists, takes the featured slot. Her own additions and the full free library sit behind one quiet link (a sheet), not on the page.
  • One header, one language. The section still (placeholder print of honeysuckle — devotion — until the Higgsfield still lands) with the title, the one heart and the flower line; clean chips; no second flower anywhere on the surface; meaning-rosettes per card kept.
