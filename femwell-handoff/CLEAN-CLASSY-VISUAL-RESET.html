A · WHAT'S WRONG NOWThe four faults, named

Before proposing the cure, here's the honest diagnosis — the specific things that read as low-rent, in your words "cream on cream" and "burnt."

  • 1 · Cream on cream. Cards (#F4EFE3) sit on a cream page (#ECE7DA) — a ~4% lightness gap. Nothing separates; the eye can't find edges, so it reads as flat and cheap.
  • 2 · The burnt overlay. Every card carries a paper-grain texture at 40% multiply plus warm brown shadows. Multiplied over cream it muddies the white and casts the whole app amber — the "burnt" cast.
  • 3 · Warm-brown text. Emphasis lines are oxblood (#7A1A12); labels are warm brown (#2E261B). Warm-on-warm lowers contrast and dates the type.
  • 4 · Everything is a box. Each element is a bordered card with a 4px coloured stripe, double shadow, flora frame and texture — five rainbow stripes down one screen. No breathing room; the opposite of restraint.

B · THE NEW LANGUAGEClean, classy, high-contrast

Six moves. Each keeps the FemWell identity — the crimson heart, the flora, the Cormorant voice — and elevates it. This section is the language, shown before → after.

B1 · Ground & surface

Swap the warm cream page for a calm alabaster ground, and make cards pure white. White on alabaster gives real, clean separation — the single move that kills "cream on cream." Cards now lift instead of blend.

B2 · Contrast & text

Retire warm-brown and oxblood text. Primary type becomes near-black ink #17140F; secondary becomes a neutral slate #6B675F. Emphasis comes from weight and the crimson heart — never from warm-red running text. High contrast, quietly.

B3 · Accent & restraint

One hero accent: the crimson heart #BC2E27, used as punctuation — a single CTA, a rule, a mark. Gold #A8893F only as a fine eyebrow. The per-card rainbow stripes (plum/sage/gold/crimson/sky all at once) are retired. Phase hues live only inside the cycle dial, where they carry data — not as card chrome.

B4 · Type scale

Same voice (Cormorant Garamond display, Ephesis for the signature flourish, system sans for chrome) — a confident role-based scale, bigger headlines, more generous body leading.

Display · 30 / 1.12
Title · 21 / 1.2
Body · 17 / 1.65 — long enough to breathe, dark enough to read at a glance.
Eyebrow · 11.5 tracked · gold
Meta · 12.5 · slate

B5 · Spacing & elevation

The biggest lever for "classy." Section rhythm goes 16 → 26px; card padding 16 → 22px. Elevation is one clean, neutral shadow — no warm double-glow, no texture, no flora frame on every card. Fewer boxes per screen. Air is the luxury.

B6 · Flora, elevated

The flora identity stays — but as a single fine botanical line-mark used once per screen (a masthead motif), drawn in ink or gold hairline. Not a muddy filled frame multiplied on every card. Clean line = elevated, not warm-and-busy.

D · FOLDED FIXESFour repairs in the same pass

D1 · CTA consolidation

On the Sky surface there were up to three CTAs stacked: the top row (Edit chart + Share) and the controller's “Open your sky” button below. “Open your sky” is self-referential — it opens the surface you're already on. Resolution: one primary CTA. The exemplar keeps Edit your chart as the single primary action and folds Share into a quiet icon in the masthead corner; the redundant “Open your sky” is removed. (You said “replace the top with the one below” — flagging that the “one below” is the self-referential one, so I've kept the genuinely useful action instead. Say the word if you'd rather I follow it literally.)

D2 · Merge Books + Story — wiring verified

Verified the plumbing. The honest finding on your “not sure it's wired correctly”: the daily Story does not run to month-end. It rotates a new chapter every single calendar day — a deterministic day-index modulo the number of chapters (dailyStory.js:81) — then silently loops back to chapter 1 after the series length, with no month boundary and no “series complete” state. There is no month/month-end logic anywhere in the story code.

One product decision for you

Which model do you want? (a) A chapter a day — current behaviour; I'd just add a clean month/series boundary instead of the silent loop. (b) One chapter held to month-end — a slower serial; not built today, I'd add the hold logic. Tell me (a) or (b) and I'll wire it in the merge.

Books is wired cleanly: FemWell fiction (LifestyleItems), free classics (Gutendex), and device-local “continue reading” — BooksFocus is purely presentational. Books and Story already share the same reader (DailyStoryReader + buildBookChapters), so the merge is presentational + routing only — no entity/schema change. Collapse the two chips into one section ordered today's chapter → continue → your shelf → free classics, and clean up the leak in D3-bug-1 below.
