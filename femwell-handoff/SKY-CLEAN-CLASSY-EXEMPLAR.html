  • Botanical dividers, not plain rules. A veined leaf-rule for the everyday break; a quatrefoil fleuron with a gold dot for the chapter-grade break before the chart. One motif per fold. §4.3 · §4.4
  • A framed feature card. The dial card carries BrandFrame corner sprigs in all four corners and a faint sage colourway wash — her phase's colour, so the tint means something. §4.2 · §6.7

  • The display voice. Astra signs her reading in Ephesis, in gold — the script as signature, not body. §1
  • Kept from the reset. Alabaster ground, white surfaces, neutral ink/slate, no paper texture, air between sections, one quiet CTA. Clean and classy — and unmistakably FemWell.
