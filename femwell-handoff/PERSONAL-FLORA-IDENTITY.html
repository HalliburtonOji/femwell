Deep brainstorm · Personal identity · 8 July 2026

Your Bloomprint

A signature flower and a living garden that grow as she does — so every woman's FemWell feels unmistakably hers, in her own pages and in the rooms.

The idea in one breath

One woman, one garden — recognisably hers, everywhere.

Every woman already has a companion bloom seeded uniquely to her account. We lift that quiet thing into a real identity: a signature flower in her colour, growing through five life-stages of her own tending, carrying gently-earned petals, rare blooms, stars and seasons. It becomes her face in the Community — unique but safe — and it lives on her Profile, Today and Garden. Not a score. A portrait that fills in as she shows up.

Why now: the whole engine is already built — this is mostly making it legible and present, then giving it a body in Community. We build on the Garden, we don't reinvent it.

1 · The identity model

The Bloomprint — deterministic, meaningful, hers.

Her identity is composed, never random — a portrait seeded from her real life. Five layers, all already in the flora system:

The maths already gives ~1.4 billion distinct gardens — no two women repeat. A returning user reads it fluently: a sunflower always means radiance, a forget-me-not always means memory, a butterfly always means you changed and came back. Meaning is the moat — 84% of wellness gamification is points-for-points with zero meaning; ours is floriography, first.

2 · What she grows (the earned layers)

Four soft layers — collection, never score.

Everything here only ever gathers. Nothing wilts, breaks, decays or can be lost. Growth is triggered by showing up and reflecting, not by output or counts.

Petals — the everyday unit

Each real act of showing up settles a petal onto her bloom — a line written, a check-in, a meal logged, a kind reply in the rooms. Petals fill toward the next growth stage. One calm daily notice ("+2 petals today"), never a nag.

Additive fill toward "in full bloom" — the empty petals are a gentle invitation, never a deficit.

Rare blooms — earned through living

The milestones already in the app become a shelf of collectibles that's only hers — a hundred journal lines, a full cycle tracked, a year grown, "not alone" (her first reach into community). Earned by living, never by hitting a target.

Stars — the rare, luminous moments

Stars are held back for the few things that deserve a hush: a return after time away, a kindness (someone held her words), a season kept. A handful of quiet lights over her night meadow — a star means the app noticed she was good to herself or to someone else. Never a currency.

Seasons — her months, each a bloom

Every month becomes a small bloom in her garden (already built as "chapters"). A year → a ring of seasons, an orchard. Seasons are time, not effort — a resting season is still a season, drawn as dormancy-with-a-bud, never as nothing.

The kind-growth promise guardrail

The rules that keep it warm, not grindy.

  • Additive only — nothing wilts, breaks or can be lost. (Loss aversion is asymmetric; a wilted bloom hurts more than a new one delights — and that's exactly the anxiety our audience avoids.)
  • Grown from showing up and reflecting, never from output or counts.
  • A gap is a resting season, met with a welcome-back — never guilt, never a broken streak.
  • Everything means something — every petal, bloom and star maps to floriography or a real moment. No points for points.
  • No leaderboards, no public tallies, no comparison. Her garden is measured only against yesterday's garden — and only if she looks.
  • Creatures visit and drift — no push-guilt. If she doesn't come, nothing bad happens; things simply wait.
3 · In the Community

Unique in the rooms — anonymous where she wants, personal where she asks.

Today a post shows a warm botanical alias ("Wild Poppy") and a plain sprout icon. We give her a face: her flower becomes her presence — so she feels like someone, not a grey blank. Femtech norm (Peanut, Flo) is layered disclosure; visual anonymity actually raises belonging for women in support spaces, and a generated flora avatar beats a photo. Three tiers, her choice per room:

How it stays safe (the reconciliation)

The Talk-rooms system remains the keeper of anonymity — this feature only decides the face. The veiled bloom is derived from the anonymous SHA-256 room-token, in a separate namespace from her account, so it can never be traced back. The Community session owns the room wiring; we hand them the presence-bloom component and coordinate via the baton.

Cross-pollination next idea

Kindness you can see, nobody exposed.

When another woman holds your words in the rooms, a bee carries a little of her colour back to your garden — an anonymous flower planted by a stranger's kindness. It inverts the streak-loss dynamic entirely: your garden gets richer from connection, not from grind, and no one is identified. (Pairs with a quiet "a kindness" star on your room-bloom.)

Sibling idea from the research: the garden gardens itself while she's away — a return after a gap finds a small wildflower has seeded itself. Absence yields a gift, never a punishment.

5 · The visitable bloom & the meadow
Expansion · approved 8 Jul — "sounds boss"

A bloom is a place — a little patch that is her.

Halli's expansion: a woman can visit another's bloom, and a bloom becomes somewhere she drops quotes, notes and activity — "like a profile page, but not." The trick is that it's meadow-native: not a corporate profile with a bio and a follower count, but a garden patch you walk up to. Profile-adjacent, garden-first.

What lives on a bloom (all curated by her)

  • Her bloom, at its true growth stage, with her companion drifting nearby — the centrepiece.
  • A pinned line — a quote or mantra she's holding right now.
  • Notes tucked in her border — a few short things she's chosen to share ("Signed up for the pottery class. Terrified. Going anyway.").
  • Her shareable shelf — the rare blooms and seasons she's earned. Safe by design: achievements, never health.
  • A guestbook — visitors can leave a kind note / plant a petal. Each note is a petal in the visitor's colour — cross-pollination made literal.

Visitable only when she opens up

A visitable patch exists only at My-flower or Named tier. A Veiled bloom still stands in the meadow — present, part of the field — but has no page: tap it and you get "a quiet bloom, keeping to herself." Her choice, always honoured. And nothing private ever appears: cycle, symptoms, moods and journal are never shown — only what she pins — and she approves every kind note before it settles. (Same whitelist-only snapshot the shared Meadow already uses — CompanionShare — extended with quote/notes/guestbook, never health.)

The recommendation

Garden and meadow are one living thing — three zooms.

The cleanest answer to "are they the same surface or linked views?" is: the same surface at different zoom levels. Nothing new to learn.

ZoomWhat it is
Your patch (close-up)your Garden page — your bloom, seasons, rare blooms, controls. Fully yours, private by default.
The meadow (wide)every opted-in patch standing together — the shared field that already exists. Anonymous presence, no ranking.
A visit (her close-up)walking up to another woman's bloom in the field → her visitable patch, gated by her tier, showing only what she curated.

So her meadow patch is her bloom page — not a separate profile bolted on. The bloom page is simply the visitor's zoom into her spot in the shared meadow. One place, seen near or far.

Build plan (phased, on Halli's yes)

  • Phase 1 — the presence bloom in Community. Swap the generic sprout chip for the veiled bloom-avatar (seeded from the room-token). One component; coordinate with the Community session. Biggest felt-uniqueness win, zero anonymity risk.
  • Phase 2 — petals + stars, derived (no new entity). Compute petals/stars from the signals the Garden already reads; surface the daily petal on Today and the identity header on Garden.
  • Phase 3 — Profile avatar becomes the signature bloom; the disclosure toggle (veiled / my-flower / named) in Community settings.
  • Phase 4 — the visitable patch + guestbook. Make the Garden the real close-up, wire the meadow tap-to-visit onto the existing CompanionShare field, extend the snapshot with pinned quote + notes + guestbook (whitelist-only, never health), and gate visitability to My-flower/Named. Guestbook notes reuse community moderation + k-anon. Needs a light data touch → spec via Mr Lead Manager + Ms Data, anonymity signed off with the Community session.
  • Phase 5 — cross-pollination + self-seeding garden (a held word plants a petal; an absence seeds a wildflower) — the two research gems, once the guestbook substrate exists.
