The Whole-Life Rebalance

Health is one room, not the house. How every surface broadens from a clinical tracker to a whole-life wellness app for women — and how they all wire together.

The correction (Halli). FemWell has been built as a health/clinical/cycle tracker. It must be a whole-life wellness app spanning relationships, dating, friendship, career, money, hobbies, fashion/beauty, creativity, identity, joy, entertainment, and community/"gossip"/venting. Two failures to fix: (a) we only ever wire 2–3 surfaces (always the clinical ones) and ignore the rest; (b) features hammer the clinical — even the games are cycle quizzes. This plan accounts for every surface and broadens each.

The evidence in one line: women don't return to an app for tracking — they return for belonging, voice, identity and lightness. Even Peanut (a TTC app) found 60% of users use the community, not the matcher; The Skimm wins on being "a smart friend texting you"; 73% prefer warm over clinical design and 37.5% abandon apps that feel "too clinical." (Sources at the end.)

1 · The two standing rules (now in CLAUDE.md)

RULE 1 — span life, not just health. No feature is clinical-only. Lighthearted by default; life-stage gently tints, never dominates.

RULE 2 — wire ALL surfaces, every time. Every cross-page design names a role for each of: Today · Journal · Community · Nutrition · Lifestyle (For You / Read / Listen / Daily Story / Horoscope) · Health · Pulse/Trends/Insights · Planner · Profile · Programs · Doctor Export · Jess · Events · Deals.

Structural finding: the whole-life capability is concentrated inside the Lifestyle hub + Events + Journal, while the surfaces a user hits FIRST (Today, Planner, the Pulse/Trends/Insights trio, Health) are 70–98% clinical. The rebalance lever is mostly (a) collapse the triple symptom-analytics consoles, (b) de-cycle-frame Today/Planner/Profile/Community, and (c) promote the already-whole-life surfaces out of their buried positions — not net-new building.

3 · Per-page rebalance — every surface

For each: today → broaden, with concrete non-clinical features + which domains it picks up.

Today 70 → ~45

Today opens on a cycle banner + symptom logging. Broaden: the front door becomes a "smart-friend" daily brief (Skimm tone) that mixes a cycle note with a Question of the Day, a Daily Story next-chapter card, a friend/Circle nudge, a "win to celebrate", a Horoscope peek, an Event near you, and one money/career micro-tip — cycle is one card, not the whole stack. Domains: fun, entertainment, friendship, career, money, identity.

Journal 45 → ~30

Already ships a "Wholeness" tag set (Relationships, Career, Creativity, Money, Grief, Joy, Identity) but they're tags only. Broaden: give each domain its own prompts + Cycle-Mirror-style resurfacing + a "share to the right room" path; add friendship & fun as first-class; let a journal entry become a Lounge vent, a Circle post, or a book-club note (the "one entry, four lives" extended to whole-life). Domains: all 12 — Journal is the private seed of every public room.

Community 45 → ~25

Live tab is a 4/6-health-tag forum. Broaden into life-domain rooms (see §5): a warm anonymous Lounge (vent/AIBU/spill-it), interest-first Circles (dating, career, money, style, hobbies, fun — not only conditions), Ask the Room (relationships/career/money), Style & Self-Expression (advice not hot-or-not), and the Shared-Experience pillar's non-clinical games (§6). Health circles remain — as some rooms among many. Domains: all 12.

Nutrition 55 → ~40

Macro/calorie/hydration spine with hormone framing. Broaden: food-as-joy & culture — a Recipe Joy Swap (share a comfort dish for the story, not the macros), dining-out & "what to cook for friends", budget/£ filters, cuisine exploration, a community cookbook (collective build). Keep macro logging for those who want it — opt-in, not the front. Domains: joy, friendship, money, creativity, culture.

Lifestyle hub + subdivisions 35 → ~20 (amplify the assets)

  • Daily Story / BookReader (0–5%) — the joy engine. Promote it to a first-class destination; expand serialized fiction, add a Book Club (the Community pillar) on top of the existing reader.
  • Listen (25%) — podcasts/TikTok/video, already broad but buried; surface on Today; add non-health shows (culture, comedy, money, careers).
  • Horoscope (20%) — fun/identity; lean into compatibility, "ask the sky", archetype play; keep the cycle-moon tie as a light flourish, not the point.
  • Read (30%) — broaden the taxonomy beyond "Women's Health / Hormones / Menopause" to include relationships, career, money, style, culture, fiction.
  • For You (40%) — the feed should weight interests (a taste profile) as much as cycle phase.

Domains: entertainment, identity, interests, fun, culture, money, career.

Health · Pulse · Trends · Insights 90–98 → consolidate

This is the clinical core and should stay clinical — but it's three near-identical symptom-analytics consoles (Pulse/Trends/Insights) plus a content hub. Rebalance = consolidate the trio into ONE "Health" room reachable from the menu (not a primary slot), so the clinical surface area stops dominating the app's first impressions. Health is one well-lit room — accurate, NHS-grounded — not the whole house. Domain: health (deliberately).

Planner 80 → ~50

A real life-planner buried under HRT logs, kick counters, EPDS, fertile-window cards. Broaden: the "capacity" frame becomes whole-life energy (social plans, hobbies, creative projects, career goals, friend dates, "me time") with the cycle as one input among many; plan an Event, a book-club checkpoint, a vision-board goal. Clinical modules stay for the stages that need them — collapsed, not front-and-centre. Domains: friendship, career, creativity, fun, relationships.

Profile 65 → ~35

Identity = reproductive stage + conditions. Broaden into "Me / My Shelf" (taste identity): currently reading/watching/listening, hobbies, "into right now", values, an archetype-for-fun — a non-clinical identity that invites connection (Letterboxd model). Life-stage stays, as one facet. Domains: identity, interests, entertainment, self-expression.

Programs 75 → ~55

Every track is a symptom remedy. Add non-clinical programmes: confidence, money/work, creativity (a 14-day write/make), friendship/social, style/wardrobe, joy. Domains: career, money, creativity, confidence, friendship.

Jess (AI) 60 → ~40

Framed as a health companion + symptom voice-logger. Broaden: Jess is the warm life confidante + host — she hosts the QOTD/book-club/games, gives non-condescending money/career/relationship guidance, and vents-with-you (never just-fixes). Health logging stays, as one of her hats. Domains: all 12 — Jess is the connective voice.

Events 10 · Deals 45 → ~30

Events is already a whole-life asset (dating/nightlife/networking/culture) — promote it and wire it into Today/Planner/Circles. Deals — broaden beyond supplements/period-care to fashion/beauty/home/hobby/books, framed as joy not just health commerce. Domains: social, dating, culture, fashion, hobbies.

Doctor Export 100 — stays

Purpose-built clinical hand-off; correctly clinical. Contract: it reads clinical/journal data only and excludes all whole-life community content (Lounge vents, Circle posts, book-club notes, voice notes) — that content is anonymous/social and never enters a GP record. (Carried from the Community §6.6 export contract.)
