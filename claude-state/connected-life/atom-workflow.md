## One worksheet for each control, state or tiny interaction

1. **Now:** routed source/file/handler and current pixel; full preservation inventory.
2. **Truth:** canonical item, owner, reads/writes, ID namespace/version, deployed schema, current evidence level. Who else consumes it?
3. **Job:** actual user trigger and useful outcome. What decision does this help?
4. **Relationship:** same object, explicit preference, accepted time, measured remaining duration or evidenced observation. Explain the join; phase similarity alone is insufficient.
5. **Personal loop:** source→action→private state→exact source/Today/Planner return. Name reverse consumer, not just outgoing link.
6. **Other-person loop:** sender→preview/audience→recipient control→response→source update/return, or “none useful”. No automatic private-data socialisation.
7. **Alternatives:** two or three distinct mechanisms/placements, each with cost and benefit; focused primary research before code. Choose a small experiment, record why.
8. **Presentation:** before/after composition, conditions for appearing, action language, loading/empty/error/stale/offline/deleted/denied states; added taps/decisions/height. Keep frequent actions direct, all functions reachable. Do not invent a numerical density rule that removes features.
9. **Consent:** exact fields and identity/audience, owner/recipient permission, moderation/block/leave/revoke; notification preview; cancellation/undo. Schema delta gets a concrete review before rollout.
10. **Proof:** actual taps at 360/390/430, loading/failure/empty/reload/source-return, desktop emulation versus real iPhone clearly stated; authorised two-account proof for social state. Independent P0/P1/P2 catalogue and re-proof.
11. **Canon:** relevant Bible §§ named; new decision same-cycle Bible/mirror/STATUS update; Ideas preview/research/known gaps. Main only on explicit Halli go-ahead.
12. **Next:** evidence just learned, what changed in the map and the single next atom. Re-brainstorm when evidence changes; no copy-paste universal bar.
