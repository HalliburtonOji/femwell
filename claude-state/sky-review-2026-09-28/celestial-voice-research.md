## Five decisions for the build

1. **Make the Moon the beautiful, truthful centrepiece.** Propose a sculpted silver disc, delicate surface marks, a restrained halo and botanical framing. Its illuminated area and curved shadow boundary follow the calculated phase. NASA describes an approximately 29.5-day cycle; waxing grows, waning shrinks, and quarter phases appear half-lit. Label our schematic as a northern view; do not claim an exact local sky angle or moonrise without observer data. The art direction is ours, the phase facts are NASA's. [NASA phases](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moon-phases/)

2. **Let each phase teach one thing, inline.** Eight compact phase buttons select a larger illustration, phase name and one short fact; a separate “Today” marker prevents mistaking exploration for the actual date. Example: “First quarter: halfway lit, a quarter of the way around.” Keep the lunar and personal-cycle markers visibly distinct. No automatic motion, compulsory tutorial or extra navigation layer. NASA supplies the phase sequence; focused, concise explanation follows Mailchimp's education guidance. [NASA phases](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moon-phases/) · [Mailchimp education](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/writing-educational-content/)

3. **Put wonder beside fact, with clear labels.** “Sky note” explains astronomy; “A thought to carry” offers an authored interpretation. Example pair: “Moonlight is sunlight reflected towards us.” / “Borrow a little light: ask someone whose judgement you trust.” Do not call newly invented lines ancient folklore. A genuine cultural story needs its specific source. Neither astronomical precision nor mythical language can establish personal health, fertility or relationship predictions. This separation applies brand §10.4 to NASA's illumination explanation. [NASA phases](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moon-phases/)

4. **Use a human voice with a short first layer.** Propose a brief heading, one useful sentence, then optional detail; retain full readings where reading is the task. Keep warmth constant while tone responds to the moment. Dry humour belongs in calm, low-stakes moments, never a failed save, distress or private-data action. Mailchimp prioritises clarity and warns against forced jokes. Brand §10.4 still limits actual omens; ordinary warmth need not become another omen. [Mailchimp voice](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/)

5. **Keep action labels literal.** Put poetry in the supporting line, and a clear verb/object on the control: “Read today's sky”, “Save reflection”, “Edit birth details”. Unknown time stays unknown; do not imply precision merely because a chart looks finished. Prefer exact save/error results over decorative reassurance. Mailchimp recommends concise verb-led buttons and descriptive links. [Mailchimp web elements](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/web-elements/)
