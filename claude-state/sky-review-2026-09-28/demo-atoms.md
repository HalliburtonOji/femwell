2. **Reading → reflection:** complete authored sample, suggested gentle action, editable reflection sheet, keep/edit/remove in session, specific visible read-back. No generated claim about Halli.
3. **Chart:** clear Sun/Moon/rising meanings, known/unknown-time switch. Unknown time removes time-dependent certainty; editable sample setup and explicit outcome.
4. **Your rhythms:** sample month/date selection; honest empty history option; biological cycle separated from lunar observation. No causal or fabricated history claims.
5. **Longer arcs:** illustrated sample year/transit explanations with dates and optional planning preview, never real Planner writes.
6. **Ask:** suggested questions and free text, visibly scripted sample reply, loading/error/retry states; no pretend personalised inference.
7. **Connection:** choose sample person/context; narrative compatibility with useful conversation prompt, no relationship score or verdict; preview sharing without personal data.
8. **Atelier/settings:** readable sample contents, explicit sample entitlement; no checkout. Quiet preference demonstrably changes session presentation; undo/reset.
