# Research Brief — User-Created Groups & Fixed-Topic "Rooms" for an Anonymous Women's-Health App (UK)

> Prepared 2026-06-11 for the FemWell Groups/Library/Games brainstorm (feeds `GROUPS_LIBRARY_GAMES_BRAINSTORM.html`). Every substantive claim is cited inline (source + URL). Unverified items are flagged. No invented statistics.

## 1) User-Created Group Community Patterns

### Facebook Groups
- Any user creates a Group. **Public groups searchable on/off Facebook**; **Private groups** still listed in search (name/description/member count visible) but posts hidden until approved (Facebook Help — https://www.facebook.com/help/220336891328465).
- **Roles:** Admin (full control) + Moderator (content/member actions, cannot change settings or assign roles) (Facebook Help — https://www.facebook.com/help/901690736606156; Guiding Tech — https://www.guidingtech.com/facebook-group-admin-roles/).
- Admin tooling: Keyword Alerts, pinned Rules/Announcements, post approval, Admin Assist (Groupboss — https://groupboss.io/blog/facebook-group-admin-tools/).
- A user may belong to **max 6,000 groups** (Facebook Help — https://www.facebook.com/help/199554316755501/). Orphaned/admin-less groups are a documented abandonment failure mode.

### Discord
- Free server creation; join via invite or Discovery. **Granular role hierarchy** with per-channel overrides (Discord — https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/214836687).
- Servers up to ~2.5M members; a user in **max 100 servers** (secondary: bestfriendsclub.ca/discord-member-limit/; thegamer.com/discord-june-update-server-size-limit-cap-2-5-million/ — Discord's own page 403'd, treat as approximate).
- **Layered moderation:** AutoMod (keyword/spam auto-actions + alerts to a private mod channel) + human mods + central Trust & Safety (Discord AutoMod FAQ — https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/4421269296535; Discord — https://discord.com/safety/our-approach-to-content-moderation). 20M+ unwanted messages auto-removed post-AutoMod (Discord blog — https://ptb.discord.com/blog/new-anti-spam-raid-automod-safety-update). Raid defence: pause invites + Report Raids.

### Reddit
- Any user creates a subreddit; volunteer user-mods + AutoModerator + community rules. **Defines "abandoned"/"unmoderated" communities and runs r/redditrequest to reassign inactive ones** (Reddit Help — https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/29187257932308). Directly relevant to the "dead group" risk.

### Geneva / Circle.so / Mighty Networks
- Geneva: rooms-by-format community app; specific caps/lifecycle **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- Circle.so: paid, host-built **Spaces/Space Groups**, paywalled sub-spaces (Mighty Networks — https://www.mightynetworks.com/encyclopedia/circle).
- **Mighty Networks: four visibility settings — Public / Private / Secret / Paid** (Mighty Networks Help — https://faq.mightynetworks.com/en/articles/9140691). Clean reference taxonomy for FemWell room/group types.

### Bumble For Friends (Hives) / Peanut
- Bumble Hives: group chat, polls, video, **group meetup planning** incl. "Games night" preset (TechCrunch — https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/15/bumble-experiments-with-group-chats-polls-and-video-calls-for-its-new-social-networking-feature-hive/; bumble.com/join-hives).
- **Peanut (most analogous):** women across life stages incl. peri/menopause; **Groups** by location/life-stage/due-date/interest; **Pods** = live moderated audio (hosts moderate, invite speakers, raise-hands). **Identity-verification-first, NOT anonymity-first** (Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut_App). Key contrast — FemWell is anonymity-first.

### Book apps
- **Fable:** celebrity/author/BookTok-hosted OR **user-created** clubs; **spoiler-free chapter rooms**, **Social Reading Mode** (live highlights/reactions), chapter discussion-prompt PDFs, free + premium ($5.99–9.99/mo) (Book Riot — https://bookriot.com/fable-book-club-app-review/; fable.co).
- **StoryGraph:** **user-created clubs** (host needs Plus); book selection, scheduled meetings, question bank, **page/percentage-locked comments to prevent spoilers**, public/private (StoryGraph — https://roadmap.thestorygraph.com/features/posts/users-can-create-groups-book-clubs-on-the-storygraph).
- **Goodreads:** forum-style user Groups, discussion boards, less structured cadence (Ross Library — https://rosslibrary.org/clash-of-the-book-apps-goodreads-fable-storygraph-and-uncover/).

## 2) Safety & Moderation of User-Led Spaces
- **Mod burnout / invisible labor:** 126 subreddits / 900+ mods — visible-only metrics "drastically underestimate" mod labor (arXiv 2205.14529 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14529). Unpaid Reddit moderation valued ≥$3.4M/yr, ≈466 hrs/day (Northwestern — https://news.northwestern.edu/stories/2022/05/unpaid-social-media-moderators).
- **Why mods quit:** too little time + conflict with other mods; exposure to profanity/slurs/threats (search-excerpt level — paraphrase).
- **Dead-group problem:** Reddit's abandoned/unmoderated definitions + reassignment exist precisely because inactivity degrades quality (Reddit Help, above).
- **Host power-misuse:** concentrated unilateral admin power (FB admins ban/delete; Discord owners sweeping permissions) = structural risk of one bad unvetted host.
- **Safeguards:** AutoMod + human mods + central T&S escalation (Discord); raid/brigading defence (pause invites); reassignment on inactivity (r/redditrequest); keyword watchlists (FB). Co-host requirements / member-to-mod ratios advocated but **no platform-mandated numeric ratio verified [UNVERIFIED]**; team moderation support is solid.

## 3) UK Online Safety Act 2023 — user-led spaces
- **FemWell is a user-to-user (U2U) service, in scope. The PLATFORM, not the volunteer host, carries the duties + liability for content in member-run groups.**
- No small-service exemption — applies "regardless of the service's size and composition"; Ofcom names discussion forums/chat rooms as in-scope; ~100,000+ providers covered (Bristows — https://inquisitiveminds.bristows.com/post/102k11s/...; Burges Salmon — https://www.burges-salmon.com/our-thinking/online-safety-act-who-does-it-apply-to/).
- **Core U2U duties:** illegal-content risk assessment (17 priority types); proportionate steps to prevent + swiftly remove illegal content; easy reporting + complaints; content-moderation systems; children's-access assessment; governance with a **named accountable person** (Ofcom — https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/illegal-content-duties-under-the-online-safety-act; Codes of Practice PDF — https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/online-safety/information-for-industry/illegal-harms/illegal-content-codes-of-practice-for-user-to-user-services-24-feb.pdf).
- **Dates:** illegal-content duties in force 17 Mar 2025; RA deadline 16 Mar 2025; children's-access 16 Apr 2025 (CMS — https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/online-safety-act-illegal-content-duties-are-now-in-force).
- **Penalties:** up to £18M or 10% qualifying worldwide revenue; access restriction; possible personal/criminal liability (Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_Safety_Act_2023; Open Rights Group — https://www.openrightsgroup.org/publications/online-safety-act-a-guide-for-organisations-working-with-the-act/).
- **Liability for member-run groups = explicitly yes;** delegating hosting to a member does NOT transfer the duty. Private/closed groups NOT exempt.
- **Children's-access:** 18+ still needs a documented children's-access assessment (age assurance).
- **ICO/UK GDPR:** health posts = Article 9 special-category; need Art 6 + Art 9 condition; **explicit consent** the likely route (health/social-care condition needs a confidentiality-bound professional — poor fit for peer chat) (ICO — https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/).

## 4) Anonymity Within Small Groups
- **Tension:** anonymity weakens accountability; hard to trace/exclude misbehavers (arXiv 1004.3057 — https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.3057).
- **k-anonymity erosion:** smaller group → smaller k; quasi-identifiers (life stage + due date + location + condition) can re-identify (Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-anonymity; ScienceDirect — https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/computer-science/k-anonymity). FemWell's 11 life stages + small group + free-text health detail shrinks the anonymity set.
- **Pseudonymity is a spectrum** (arXiv 1211.5613 — https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.5613). Reddit throwaways = candour + content-level moderatability (arXiv 2501.17430, title-level). Peanut = opposite (mandatory ID). FemWell sits between → **stable pseudonyms + platform-side identity link held only for escalation.**

## 5) Book Club / Shared-Activity Group UX
- Host-led AND self-organising both supported (Fable). StoryGraph clubs user-created/host-run.
- Cadence: scheduled meetings + question bank (StoryGraph); chapter-by-chapter prompt PDFs (Fable).
- **Spoiler management solved:** Fable spoiler-free chapter rooms; StoryGraph page/%-locked comments. Adopt progress-gated discussion.
- Social Reading Mode = lightweight co-presence (Fable).
- **Engagement trap:** a 2,000-member club had only ~20 comments in its kickoff section (Book Riot). **Big membership ≠ active discussion;** small prompted host-nudged cohorts win.

## Top 12 Takeaways
1. The platform (not the host) is legally liable under OSA — moderate centrally.
2. Adopt Mighty-Networks visibility taxonomy (Public/Private/Secret/[Paid]).
3. Plan for abandonment day one — Reddit-style inactivity flag + reassignment/hand-off.
4. Require ≥2 co-hosts for member-run groups (burnout + single-point abandonment).
5. Layer moderation: AutoMod-style filters + human + central escalation (Discord).
6. Stable pseudonyms, not raw anonymity; identity link platform-side for escalation only.
7. Guard k-anonymity in small groups (min sizes, discourage location precision, warn before disclosure).
8. Health posts = Article 9; explicit consent, minimise storage.
9. Documented children's-access assessment even at 18+.
10. Engagement ≠ membership; favour small prompted host-nudged cohorts.
11. Build spoiler/progress gating into Library/book-club rooms.
12. Give Jess (AI host) a structural role: default host for fixed rooms + fallback host for abandoned member groups (synthesis, not external claim).

## Patterns to AVOID
- ID-verification-first as the only safety lever (incompatible with anonymity-first).
- Solo unvetted hosts with unilateral power.
- Assuming private/closed groups are out of OSA scope (they aren't).
- Delegating moderation entirely to hosts (illegal under OSA + burnout generator).
- Chasing big group size as a success metric.
- Group proliferation with no lifecycle policy.
- Tight micro-segmentation + free-text health detail in tiny groups (k-anonymity erosion).
- No spoiler/progress gating in shared-reading rooms.
