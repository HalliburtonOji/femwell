## Research that changes the design

- [ElevenLabs' current product guide](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/playground/text-to-speech) distinguishes high-quality v4 from low-latency v4 Turbo and warns that voice settings are nondeterministic; extreme speed or exaggerated style can worsen quality. Voice source and edited script matter before a slider. Its audio effects can add Noise/Space/Distance, but an effect is not a substitute for acting.
- [ElevenLabs' speech best practices](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/best-practices) say punctuation and structure control pauses and that audio tags can be misread as sounds. The editorial rule is to audition the **finished render**, not trust a prompt saying “human”.
- [ElevenLabs' streaming guide](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/concepts/audio-streaming) distinguishes model inference latency from time to first audible sound, including network and buffer. Dynamic Jess needs measured end-to-end response, cancellation and text fallback; a vendor latency figure does not certify the app.
- [ElevenLabs' billing overview](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/intro) says TTS consumes credits by input character. The app should generate each approved editorial voice/edition once and reuse it; interactive Jess turns need an explicit bounded budget and shared cache where text is truly identical.
- [W3C media planning](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/planning/) and [transcript guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/transcripts/) support user-controlled playback and a transcript including meaningful sounds. Keep voice-off and reduced-motion paths first-class.

## Content and presence: the spoken thing has a job

**One small, recognisable doorway:** an ear/voice control on the relevant piece and a compact player after tap. Do not add a new voice shelf under every header. The current room's one spoken edition can also appear at the Listen desk under the **same ID/version**, and an exact Keep leads back to its origin. Jess's name/voice can sit at the edge of a relevant piece as “Hear it from [name]”; she must not start talking when a page opens, when the user scrolls, or when another player is active. A pinned, low-noise player may survive route changes with existing media controls; Close and pause work immediately.

| Place | Worth hearing | Truth and return |
|---|---|---|
| Read | A 45–90-second magazine *margin dispatch* attached to one complete article. Example: an **authored fiction** vignette about a woman missing a bus, a wet shoe and the absurd trip home; or a named editor reading a checked article's actual observation. | Fiction labelled as fiction. News/factual audio names the original source, publication/recheck date and editor; the direct action opens the full article. Do not turn a social post into an unverified “fact”. |
| Listen | The one current short edition and the full episode she was already playing, without another short-content catalogue. | Shared edition ID, real duration, full transcript, source and direct return. No 20-second external Short dressed up as an episode. |
| Books | A brief original introduction or licensed/public-domain scene for the actual book/chapter. | Open reader at the exact chapter; never narrate unlicensed commercial text. Reader place and short-listen Keep stay separate. |
| Sky | Tiny mythical-in-the-kitchen or everyday-observatory stories; the moon can be cheeky, not a horoscope impersonating fact. | Preserve Bulletin swipe, note, chart, astronomy and source. A story is fiction; the sky fact panel remains sourced optional depth. |
| Good life | Room-specific scene followed by one doable action if useful; e.g. Move's chosen “not today” never voices a workout. | Match room/topic, keep the full current choices/tools and Plan acknowledgement. No implied health result. |
| Yours | Her exact saved editions and continuing audio. | No synthetic summary *about her*, no fresh generation on opening a save, no lost historical source. |
| Jess / adjacent app | Contextual **invitation** into the current task (“Want me to talk this through?”), then concise interactive speech on tap. | Read only consented context, disclose AI speech, show/transcribe every answer, stop/cancel/mute, don't recite private journal or health details in public by default. Other app pages can adopt the same entry grammar after their own full click/owner audit. |

**Social apps:** a future *public-culture bulletin* can explain a genuinely sourced public trend, with link, date, source count and the plain phrase “People are saying this; it hasn't been verified.” Public posts are leads, never the evidence for medical, legal or money guidance. No private DMs, contacts or Community posts become story fodder; no voice reads them aloud without a deliberate user action. Ask for a concrete Ideas decision on source/rights, freshness and moderation before building an automated feed. A funny social aside belongs in leisure content, not a fabricated claim about a user.

**Human sound as direction, not a trick:** record clean speech first. The scene layer may carry a single chair creak, coat rustle, bus brake, page-turn or kitchen clink at the point the script actually calls for it; place it under the spoken line with headroom and let silence work. A breath or tiny laugh may come from an actual performance. A cough is an occasional character/story beat, not a random synthetic tic inserted into Jess's live answers. Keep clinical/private guidance dry and clear. Give the user a “voice only” mix option and include meaningful non-speech sounds in the transcript. Do not imply a human is secretly present: label the companion as AI, while making its *delivery* warm and believable.

## Build order, gates and cost

**V01 built locally; release proof pending:** ten same-script audition assets, a designed mobile Ideas room, exact listening/selection/return proof, this source map, Bible direction. No main, account preference, backend, social ingestion or AI reply changed.

**V02 decision after Halli auditions:** choose one preferred voice *direction* and whether all ten remain offered at launch. The ten-voice user choice is Halli's requested product direction; the decision is which actual performances pass and how much sound design they need. Do not infer that the ten audition names are final personas.

**V03 shared identity/settings:** audit all `UserProfile.jess_name`, `.ai_assistant_name`, auth `User.voice_id`, Jess/Guide settings/onboarding consumers and old saved editions. Propose one canonical owner with migration/fallback and rights/cost review. A schema/default change needs a concrete Ideas approval. Preserve old settings/action paths until they are really migrated.

**V04 editorial engine:** one edition registry with immutable ID/version, a chosen-voice asset map, transcript and source/rights/recheck state. Render ten versions only for a *finished approved* edition, once each, with QA and no synthesis on Play. The old pilot and every Keep remain. A 14-edition two-day backlog at ten voices would mean 140 audio renders, so catalogue, bytes, egress and review effort must be measured before turning on rotation. No scheduling claim from a single pilot.

**V05 Jess speech path:** retain the existing Jess agent, action envelope, recognition, transcript/history, rename and privacy settings. Replace device-dependent *output* behind an allowlisted existing dispatcher only after an explicit cost/latency/privacy design review; don't create a 51st function. Bound length, concurrent calls, retry count and timeouts; cancel old speech, fall back to text, never send API keys to the browser. Measure turn-to-first-audio and billed characters on real devices. Reconcile the separate OpenAI Guide voice mode deliberately, not by breaking onboarding.

**V06 contextual/social:** add one optional doorway per mapped owner; public social bulletin only after sourcing/rights/moderation decision. Complete old-paper opened-state cleanup and 360/390/430 + iPhone listen/interrupt/route/Back/Save/voice-off proof for each owner.

Capacity model: twelve 171-character test submissions total 2,052 input characters; ten retained static files total 993,496 bytes. Future approved edition × ten voices is a one-time provider cost; playback is hosted bytes, not TTS. Dynamic Jess costs per *novel* reply and can become dominant at scale: five 150-character replies/day × 100,000 listeners would submit 75 million characters/day **before** provider caching or text-only usage, an illustrative work count, not a bill or user-capacity claim. Instrument real turn frequency, characters, media bytes, storage and failure/retry work before setting defaults. Do not autoplay, prefetch ten files, synthesize every user/page visit, or build an unbounded “always talking” feed.
