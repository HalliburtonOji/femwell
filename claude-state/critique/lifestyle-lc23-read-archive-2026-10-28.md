## Mapped repair and research

- **R09a / first choice:** main Read may show a real saved continuation first. A generated factual archive must not silently wear a fresh edited-magazine label. Give a true reviewed choice if present; otherwise use a short honest handoff to Browse while preserving every older full item. Ideas still leads with its clearly unreviewed original issue.
- **R09b / Browse:** archive rows keep their exact Read action, full body, source exit, Details, Keep and return. Put an explicit provenance cue where the user chooses, without a wall of warning text.
- **R09c / opened owner:** `/LifestyleDetail` must label generated archive provenance rather than lending `FemWell Editorial` as a human-edited byline. Its full text, relative date, category, source URLs, position, Keep, Like, related published links and return survive.
- **R09d / proof:** scoped regression tests, production build, real 360/390/430 open/tap/close, P0/P1/P2 audit, independent bounded review, reproof, Ideas receipt and live hash.

[ONS content design](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/content/writing-for-users/user-needs) puts user need, concise structure and peer/stakeholder review before publication. [GOV.UK UI writing](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/writing-for-user-interfaces) supports a literal, short provenance cue rather than another long disclaimer. These justify the interface direction; they do not establish clinical truth for the old articles. Factual claims in the old generated health/finance pieces still need a qualified human and current primary-source check before editorial promotion.
