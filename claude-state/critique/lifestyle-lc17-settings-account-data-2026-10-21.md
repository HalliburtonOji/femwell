## Actual journey and complete first inventory

Signed-in, the live Profile Account board's **Data export → Open data controls** tap lands at `/Settings?section=privacy`; the Privacy section does render. It is a visibly older dark-brown Settings owner, immediately after the clean Profile sheet. The route is `src/pages/Settings.jsx`. Its five section buttons are Account, Notifications, Privacy, Data and About. Account edits display name, birthday, city and bio; Notifications edits five preferences; Privacy contains three visibility toggles plus separate analytics, six consent categories, reminders, a 21-category export and deletion; Data offers another five-entity JSON export and a 12-month CSV; About links terms/privacy. `src/components/layout/MenuSheet.jsx` also sends Settings here and carries its own quick tiles, five pillars, four community links, three account links, Jess, sign-out, swipe close and focus handling. These are separate owners; no one-page polish claim covers them.

The UI currently tells her the Privacy export includes **everything**, but `src/components/DataExportCard.jsx` has a fixed 21-entity list. Each read runs concurrently over all owner rows; each failed read is converted to `[]`, so a partially empty file can still display “ready”. The HTML companion counts categories but holds no row contents; the JSON is the actual selected dataset. The Data-tab JSON is a second, narrower five-entity implementation and also converts failed reads to empty arrays. It calls that a **complete copy**. No pagination, measured row/byte work, destination read-back or production cost evidence has yet been established. These are truthful-scope and failure-state defects, not proof of a legal violation.

The live Privacy deletion card says it permanently deletes **all** data and account information. `src/components/compliance/AccountDeletionCard.jsx` actually reads/deletes 21 named data entities in a client-side all-settled cascade, then only the selected `UserProfile` row, signs out and redirects to `/Privacy?account=deleted`. Failed reads are treated as empty; failed deletes are counted internally, yet the UI sets `done` and says “Your data has been removed” regardless. The component itself acknowledges that the Base44 auth row cannot be deleted by its client API. We did **not** open the final confirm, type DELETE or execute this irreversible path. The existing self-service path and its history must be preserved until Halli chooses a safe replacement; no superficial rename, hidden button or clean skin can make partial deletion complete.

Account, Notifications and Privacy `save()` in Settings lack `try/finally` or an error view: a denied write can leave the button stuck “Saving…”. `SettingsCard` uses an older bright pink/cream shell and `SettingsToggle` an orange on-state, adding to the route discontinuity. The current mobile view stacks all five section buttons before the selected content. These are next mapped functional/visual atoms. No global primitive or Menu/nav edit may proceed without its consumer map and 360/390/430 opened-state proof.

## Focused research and design direction

- [ICO right to data portability](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-data-portability/) distinguishes the scope of portability from “every piece of data”; machine-readable format and secure handling matter. [ICO access-vs-portability guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/right-of-access/how-can-we-supply-information-to-the-requester/) makes the wider access scope explicit. Product inference: the app needs a named, accurately scoped self-download and a distinct full-data-request route, not one blanket “everything” claim.
- [ICO right to erasure](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-erasure/) expects a tracked response and proportionate identity confirmation; it recognises cases where erasure may not apply. Product inference: account closure needs a request/receipt, coverage and failure tracking, and verified completion before the UI says deleted. This is not legal advice or a proposed in-app retention policy.
- [Apple sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets), [motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion) and [W3C reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) inform one focused data-control surface, legible tap targets, exact Back/focus and reduced motion. A clean Profile-to-Settings visual handoff should remove the underlying old Settings chrome and card skins, keep the same camellia hint as a family resemblance, and place one clear task at a time. It should not become another row of five nested destination pages.

## Reviewable product choice before dependent implementation

**Recommended A — verified account-data request plus clearly scoped instant downloads.** Retain Profile, Settings Privacy and Data entrances, Doctor Export, JSON/CSV and the user's ability to request erasure. Stage a clean Settings owner in Ideas with one compact section chooser and one readable data-control area. Label instant downloads by actual included categories and abort/flag incomplete reads. Replace the unsafe self-service completion claim only after a verified request workflow exists: identity confirmation when needed, a durable request ID/status, explicit data/secondary-store coverage, auth credential handling, partial-failure reporting and a human completion check. Respect the 50-function cap by extending a mapped existing dispatcher only after architecture review. This is a substantive privacy/schema/backend decision and remains held for Halli.

**Alternative B — retain client cascade as an explicitly partial local clear.** Show exact categories and failures, never say account deleted and direct the remaining auth/other-data request to support. It preserves more immediate self-service action but still risks irreversible partial removal and many per-row calls; not recommended as the long-term account closure path.

**No-code decision default:** keep the current irreversible implementation untouched pending Halli's choice; do not test it on her account. A next bounded, independently authorised stage can still repair Settings save-error handling, accurately label *non-destructive* export scope, and prototype the clean Settings/Menu handoff in Ideas. Before shipping even that, claim each owner, map all consumers, verify actual 360/390/430 taps including error/retry/close, and cost-count reads/rows/bytes without a production load storm. Brand Bible §§2.7, 10.5.22–23, 10.5.73–74, 13.1, 17.3 and 19.7–19.9 apply. No app-wide uniformity or account-data fulfilment is certified by this brief.
