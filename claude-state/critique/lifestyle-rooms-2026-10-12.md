## Focused brainstorm before code

These are design inferences from real source/visible UI, not engagement measurements. The existing header stays. Each room carries its material into the first useful task and a few spaced transitions, never a full-page flower image or decoration over control labels.

1. **Read / iris:** the iris has upright standards and spreading falls ([RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/265633/iris-pink-panther-%28sdb%29/details)). Use a cropped, fading fold at the reading desk and an actual slim bookmark at Continue. One Ephesis “Open a page” signature marks the reading desk; the useful title and rows remain Cormorant/system. A readable direct Read action leads to full text; Details stays optional. Fresh/fictive lists get a finite opening and an explicit More disclosure without losing entries.
2. **Listen / bluebell:** native English bluebells are *Hyacinthoides non-scripta* ([RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/weeds/bluebells-as-weeds)); the drooping bell/stem suggests a restrained cadence beside the actual transport, not an invented player or waveform. “On air” is a current-player state. A short signature introduces the featured listen, then simple programme rows and real watches. Keep the real audio owner and full directory.
3. **Books / jasmine:** jasmine climbs and flowers in clusters ([RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/jasmine/growing-guide)). The existing approved table remains the header; a slender, fading vine becomes a margin/bookmark gesture at the chapter and monthly book, never repeated card furniture. Chapters/club/notes/doorway all stay. Only the capacity/index section is optional as already implemented; no silent backlog change.
4. **Good life / marigold:** calendula/marigolds have vivid daisy-like blooms ([RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/calendula/)). Make the time picker the centre of a warm “available light” composition, then concise joy entries and a single obvious room door. Its eleven rooms stay in the existing popup. Avoid repeating motivational copy and invented durations.
5. **Yours / forget-me-not:** small blue clusters with yellow/white eyes ([RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/41558/myosotis-sylvatica/details)) suit a subtle stitch/index language around actual keeps, not a fake scrapbook. Lead with exact owned keeps; discoveries remain clearly separate. Show real source distinction for same-title stories; unreadable/unavailable source keeps an honest retry.
6. **Opened state:** user task remains visible when a panel opens. Modal focus/return follows [W3C dialog guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/). “Details” alone can be mistaken for navigation, per [GOV.UK](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/details/), so keep explicit direct actions and named optional details. Carry section-specific typography and one small material accent into preview opened sheets. Replace old paper at `AgeGate` itself only on a labelled Ideas preview path; source Community remains separately held behind the untouched assurance action. Never use a new cover layer over `PAPER_BG`.
