## Research and design decision

[W3C disclosure pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/) requires a visible control and expanded state for optional detail. [Apple disclosure guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/disclosure-controls) puts the common action first and optional detail behind a clearly named disclosure. Applied here: one first-fold birth-date action, one `What you'll need` detail, a short visible date/time/place cue, and the original full chart sheet. The chart failure is distinct from an absent chart; sign-in cannot be offered as a useless retry. One selected flower tint follows the six tabs.
