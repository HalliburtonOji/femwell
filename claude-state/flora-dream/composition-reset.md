## Research → concrete decisions

- [BaseLine / Maybe Design Studio](https://maybedesignstudio.com/projects/baseline): editorial type, soft imagery and a consistent component system are designed together. Transfer: header, selector, actions and summary share one hierarchy. This case study does not establish measured usability gains.
- [Phenology / Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/phenology): botanical forms carry meaning and personality; complementary type roles and colour form a coherent system. Transfer: distinct section species and a shared photographic-painterly treatment, with restrained plum controls. No copied imagery or identity.
- [Oura product redesign](https://ouraring.com/blog/new-app-design/): emphasis follows immediate daily purpose. Transfer: actual section actions precede the full digest. No adoption of scores or new nested navigation.
