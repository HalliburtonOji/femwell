# Sky: a garden that belongs to the page

Halli rejected the five square gardens on 2 October: generic, stamped pictures that do not fit the page. Claim a719720. Sky first, in Ideas only. Prior studies retained; main held.

## Four atoms

1. Open editorial masthead: complete HTML title/heart/profile, a larger calculated Moon and asymmetric morning-glory growth, with no closed border or ground line. Optional meaning, finite movement, retry remain.
2. Continuous page ground and section controls: same spacing/colour rhythm; all icons/labels/actions, cycle context and Everything retained. Sparse plant shadows/petals continue through controlled margin space.
3. Contextual action pair: existing callbacks and chart/no-chart states, refined proportions on the same ground; immediate actions remain visible.
4. Continuous reading: full glance/Jess and inline Sky content retained; shared typographic hierarchy and colour. Earlier Garden/Almanac/Canopy remain reviewable. Other sections retain their previous treatment pending approval of this direction.

## Research informing the composition

- [Wolff Olins, NYBG](https://wolffolins.com/work/nybg): botanical photography and a conservatory-derived grid belong to one identity. Translation: arrange title, phase, navigation and actions before composing art; cropped growth implies a larger living space.
- [Pentagram, Grow to Know](https://www.pentagram.com/work/grow-to-know): botanical cyanotypes work with blueprint/masterplan organisation. Translation: fine open orbital geometry belongs to the live lunar display; tangible plants cross selected lines without crossing text.
- [Pentagram, Garden Museum](https://www.pentagram.com/work/garden-museum): expressive imagery coexists with strong identity and clear wayfinding. Translation: decrease botanical density towards controls/content, carrying one meaningful petal detail through the first fold.

These are design translations, not measured usability findings. No copied imagery or claim of exact source pixels. Ms Deep Search supplied current primary references; Ms Atelier supplied the geometry and generation brief; Mr Lead Manager supplied the no-strip inventory.

## Art direction

“A tendril borrows the Moon's path.” Cropped upper-right morning glory: pleated violet trumpets, silver-sage heart-shaped leaves, an imperfect curled tendril, coherent soft light. Separate sparse fallen-petal/shadow layer at the lower-left. Transparent edges, no moss floor, miniature landscape, wreath or gold jewellery. CSS provides shared atmosphere beyond the image bounds. Real lunar phase remains separate from decorative art.

At phone width: roughly250–270px content-driven title area, full title/profile at left,110–120px Moon upper-right; controls lead into actions and full summary. These are starting dimensions, not permission to shrink/strip content. Verify actual360/390/430 pixels and real taps, then adversarial review. Passing those checks does not imply Halli's aesthetic acceptance.
