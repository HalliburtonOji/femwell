## Fresh primary research and interpretation

Ms Deep Search checked these 6 October. Translations are design inference, not engagement claims. Original generated art, not copied museum images.

- https://www.phillipscollection.org/collection/open-window — central clearing and garden-coloured light entering the interior: reserve full live title space and carry light below.
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/286175 — arranged library still life: inhabited asymmetric reading objects, blank spines, no fake commercial shelf.
- https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/paul-gauguin-a-vase-of-flowers/ — fallen blossoms connect vase/table: different physical relations of the same species/material, not repeated stickers.
- https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/william-morris-text — content-appropriate ornament, margins, paper, type/illustration together: each existing useful surface has its own physical typology.
- https://www.getty.edu/news/making-a-medieval-manuscript-from-raw-materials-to-illuminated-masterpiece/ — ruling/illumination/binding as one process: one fine stem can emerge from a rule outside reading lanes.

- https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html and https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-accessibility/reduced-motion-evaluation-criteria — complete static composition under reduced motion; no scroll-driven depth/animated blur.
