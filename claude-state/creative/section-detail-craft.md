## Common craft boundaries

- **Typography:** approved header untouched. Body section headings use Bible Heading 1, internal labels Heading 2, object titles Title, source text Body/Body L and metadata Caption. Ephesis stays in the page title; ordinary content does not become script. Use canonical oxblood headings, near-black reading ink and semantic controls. No fractional sizes, captions below the 12px floor, or extra font family. Current inherited violations are mapped cleanup atoms, not reasons to hide content.
- **Space:** start from 16px outer gutters at 360, 16px inner feature padding, 12/16px within an object and 24/32px between named movements. An open reading lane is about 328px before inner padding. Keep marginal incidents within a 16–24px edge or outside the text plane; do not subtract a permanent ornamental column. Increase breathing room around a meaningful change of activity, not around every eyebrow. These are proposed spacing placements using the existing scale, to be checked in pixels.
- **Materials:** one meaningful incident in a viewport. Colour stays in species/material edges and the chosen feature surface; no texture overlay behind reading. Preserve alpha/fade into the clean ground. A flora detail may cross a rule, never a word or tap target. Retire an old decorative duplicate only by replacing its decorative role; capabilities remain.
- **Actions:** preserve the current focused pair. The object below has its own direct consume/DO action, rather than another list to choose the same thing. Deep readers and long answers stay complete. A short task opens the existing in-place quick action. Distinct icon-only actions require explicit accessible names and generous clear targets; aim for the app's 44px targets, not merely WCAG's 24px minimum.
- **Motion:** no parallax or endless drift. The approved finite header behaviour stays. Body motion reports an actual state, such as real playback, or a brief optional plant/light settle. Reduced motion removes non-essential movement without deleting information.

“Fold” below means the first/next **body** screen after the approved common header/chrome, not a promise that the entire feature fits above the initial screen edge.
