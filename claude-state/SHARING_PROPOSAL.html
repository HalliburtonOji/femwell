How sharing ties to the app

"People also like to share things — how can this tie to the app so people can share their personal lives on the app, or share things outside?" Two tracks, split by one hard wall: privacy. Cited research; approval checklist with effort×impact. Nothing built. 2026-06-11.

The architecture is the safety model. There are two completely separate sharing systems, and the wall between them is non-negotiable: (1) INTERNAL — sharing your personal life into safe in-app spaces (Circles, Clubs, the wall, a 1:1 witness), anonymous-first and consented; (2) EXTERNAL — sharing non-personal artifacts (a wisdom line, a horoscope, an affirmation, a book pick, an invite) out to Instagram/WhatsApp/links as a polished on-brand card. Personal or anonymous content never leaves the app. The only thing that goes external is the user's OWN non-personal words, by explicit opt-in, as a card.

THE WALL — anyone's anonymous echo · any journal entry · any witnessed disclosure · any other member's post → can NEVER leave the app by any share path.

Why the wall is regulation, not just taste

  • Anonymous health posts are re-identifiable. Authorship-attribution re-identified anonymous IVF-forum posters at 97.9% accuracy (≥300-word messages); rare-condition + reused usernames enable linkage attacks (PMC3806358; PMC7457524). Letting one member screenshot/share another's anonymous post out of the app effectively re-publishes special-category-by-inference data.
  • UK GDPR: health data is special-category — and is triggered by inference, not just explicit statements; needs an Article 6 basis + an Article 9 condition (ICO). OSA + ICO cross-over: UGC is personal data and a DPIA is required before processing (Pinsent Masons / ICO).
  • The cautionary cases are women's-health-shaped. Flo Health (FTC, 2021) shared sensitive fertility data with Facebook/Google despite privacy promises — outbound data flows are a regulator magnet for this exact app category (FTC). Strava's default-public sharing leaked military base locations — default-public is the anti-pattern (TechCrunch).

Track 2 — EXTERNAL sharing (non-personal artifacts, out to the world)

This is the growth track — and it only ever touches the curated non-personal artifact set: a Living Wisdom line, today's horoscope, an affirmation, a book-club pick, "this resonated," and the invite link. Rendered as a beautiful on-brand share-card.

The share-card system (design + branding)

  • Render server-side from a templated SVG → PNG (placeholder substitution; embed the brand fonts) — the proven pipeline behind Spotify Wrapped / Strava cards (Rowy). On Base44: a self-contained function renders the card.
  • 1080×1920, 9:16, Story-shaped, high contrast, one hero line. Brand tokens (cream/espresso/blush/sage), Fraunces + Inter, no emoji, a small wordmark + the invite (a short link or QR). Built to the social canvas (Duolingo; Meta).
  • Identity-signalling, never data-dumping — "a humble-brag wrapped in beauty" the user is proud to be seen with; never her logged health data (startupspells).
  • No PII on the card — no username, handle, or avatar (username reuse enables linkage).

The mechanisms

  • Primary: Web Share API — render the card → navigator.share({ files:[png] }) after a navigator.canShare feature-check; this surfaces Instagram, WhatsApp, Messages as targets. Must be tap-triggered + HTTPS (MDN).
  • Fallbacks (Web Share isn't universal): download-image, copy-link, and WhatsApp/Telegram text-link shares.
  • Instagram Stories caveat: the direct instagram-stories:// background-image handoff is native-only (needs a Facebook App ID + the IG app; no web path) — so on mobile web the card + navigator.share route is the realistic one (Meta).
  • Deep links back in: every shared card carries a link to the app surface it came from (a wisdom collection, the book club, the invite) — the inbound loop.

Invite / referral growth (done right)

  • Content-led, not a naked link — the invite rides a beautiful wisdom/horoscope card, not a spammy "refer 5 friends" blast. (Broadcasting links to strangers is the spam/fraud anti-pattern — Alloy.)
  • Double-sided, non-cash — both sides get something gentle (a feature, a credit), not money; wellness users respond to alignment over discounts (ReferralCandy; Yotpo).
  • Never scrape contacts; never auto-broadcast. Instrument which cards get shared (like Duolingo's screenshot tracking) to learn what spreads — Duolingo reports ~80% organic acquisition from these loops (case study).

Hard privacy rules (apply to ALL of Track 2)

  •  Two tracks, hard wall — Track-1 personal/anonymous content has zero external-share affordance.
  •  Never let one user share another user's content externally — the single hardest "never."
  •  No health/logged data ever on a card; treat any user-authored text as special-category-by-inference.
  •  Default to private; never opt a user into outbound sharing (the Strava lesson).
  •  No PII / handle / avatar on cards; no third-party analytics or marketing SDK anywhere in the share path (the Flo lesson).
  •  Only the user's OWN non-personal artifact leaves, by explicit per-share opt-in.
  •  DPIA before launch of the outbound-share feature; the OSA/ICO floor (already on the list) covers it.

The growth / marketing upside

  • The external card is the organic-growth engine — Duolingo's shareable cards drove a reported 5x–10x sharing lift and ~80% organic acquisition (startupspells). For FemWell the wisdom line + horoscope are the natural, on-brand, safe-by-construction artifacts to carry an invite.
  • It's growth without compromising the thesis — the safe container stays sealed; only the beautiful, non-personal, identity-signalling lines go out. The brand is the marketing.
