A calmer Community doorway

The home has become a wall of cards. This proposes a quieter information architecture — a doorway, not a noticeboard — now that the central Jump switcher guarantees access to everything. Pick an option; nothing gets rebuilt until you do. 2026-06-12.

The principle: the Community home is a calm doorway — a quiet masthead + ambient presence, one gentle "today" focal point, and the rooms/doors as the spine. Everything else either becomes a door, rotates through a single "today" slot, lives in the Jump hub, or demotes to a small link. Because the Jump hub now reaches every area, no secondary card needs to occupy the home.
