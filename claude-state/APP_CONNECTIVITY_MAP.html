App Connectivity Map

Every surface, and the concrete links it could have to Community, Journal, Jess, and each other. An approval checklist: tick the lines to greenlight. Each line gives the FROM→TO, the mechanism, an effort×impact tag, and a recommendation. Nothing here is built. 2026-06-11.

The core finding: the app's doing surfaces (Journal, Community, Jess) and its content surfaces (Lifestyle reads, Horoscope, Daily Story, Listen, Nutrition, Programs, Events, Deals, SkinHair) live in near-total isolation. A woman reads a beautiful piece, finishes a program day, or gets her horoscope — and there is no thread back into reflecting, discussing, or asking Jess. The single highest-leverage move is one reusable "Reflect · Discuss · Ask Jess" action bar dropped onto every content surface. Most other proposals are small variations on three mechanisms.

How to read this

Effort S small M medium L large  ·  Impact shown as High Med Low  ·  Rec DO recommended OPTIONAL LATER. EXISTS = already wired (don't re-propose). The box is the tick target.

PatternWhat it isEnabling primitive
P1 Reflect→JournalAn action that opens the Journal composer seeded with a prompt about the thing you just read/did.Journal composer already accepts a seed (used by Cycle Mirror / four-lives). Add a /Journal?compose=1&seed=… deep-link handler.

P4 Content→QOTD/Echo seedEditorial content (a read, a horoscope) seeds the day's Community talking point.QOTD is a static rotation today; add an editorial seed source.
P5 Aggregate belongingA count-free "others in your season notice this too" line that links to Community — never exposes the user's data.Reuse the SeasonCard framing (already built on Community home).
P6 Interests→CirclesMap onboarding followed_categories to suggested Circles/Clubs.Interests captured at onboarding; Circles are a static catalogue — a simple map.

The keystone: P1+P2+P3 packaged as one "Reflect · Discuss · Ask Jess" component (the action bar) is the build that unlocks ~15 of the lines below. Approving it once is the big decision.

  • Doctor's Export stays isolated — no Community/Echo/Witness/Twin in; no peer content out.
