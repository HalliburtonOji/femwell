Why do I sleep so badly the week before my period?
Mostly, it's the progesterone drop — and here's what we actually know.
1
Progesterone raises core body temp ~0.5°C. When it drops at late luteal, sleep architecture fragments.1
2
Progesterone modulates GABA-A receptors — crash causes sleep-onset anxiety and early-morning waking.2
3
REM sleep is particularly disrupted in late luteal for ~65% of cycling women.3
For you: last four luteal weeks averaged 5.3h sleep. Follicular averaged 7.1h. Gap of 1.8h.

Appendix 04 · Guardrails — applied to every v2 surface

The 5 won'ts and 2 wills

✕ 01
Daily budget shared across proactive features. Ambient + Digest + Pre-Panic share ~3 touches in 24h. Users never spammed.
✕ 02
Never diagnose, prescribe, or interpret meaning. Applies to Witness, Teach Me, everything. Quote data back, don't explain it.
✕ 03
Never send anything on the user's behalf. Jess Draft is copy-only — no send tool is ever wired.
✕ 04
Never ask consent twice after a no. If she declined memory for Mum, no re-ask. Respect the no forever.
✕ 05
Never fabricate citations. Teach Me cuts any claim that can't be traced to Programs, AMAs, or papers.
✓ 01
Always show the signals used. Every proactive ping names the data points. No mystery intelligence.
✓ 02
Always offer an equally-weighted skip. Every "unpack" sits next to "not now" or "let it go."
LD recommendation · April 2026

Jess v2 is the full vision. Ship three first.

All ten features fit the existing identity. None of them violate the voice brief or the guardrails. But shipping all ten in Q2 is unrealistic. The recommendation is to ship the three highest-leverage, lowest-infra additions first — they turn Jess from signed-off-v1 into daily-habit Jess inside nine weeks.

Q2 · ship first
Morning Digest · Pre-Panic · Draft
Highest leverage, no new infra required beyond what's already in Jess v1 + Pulse.
Q3 · earn the voice
Ambient · Teach Me · Witness · Relational
Requires pattern engine, citation tagging on Programs/AMAs, relational graph.
Q4 · ride the infra
Companion Voice · Week Shaper · Presence
Voice TTS partner, calendar OAuth, all on top of previous quarters' work.

Once all three quarters ship, Jess becomes the reason FemWell is sticky — not the cycle tracking, not the wearable sync, not the Community. The noticing companion is the moat.
