Entity + integration notes

Entities introduced: PractitionerDirectory (regulator, licence_no, region, specialty, availability, last_verified_at, auto_unlist_on_suspension) · SeasonalFoodLibrary (produce, uk_season_months, nhs_eatwell_mapping, phase_tags, advisor_id) · JessVoiceVariant (region, accent_code, artist_credit, equity_band, sample_lines[]) · WellbeingMoment (date_rule, moment_type, surface_cap, per_user_opt_in).

Regulator integrations (must verify before Q1/27 ship): GMC live register API, NMC NMC-Online check, HCPC online register, BDA members' directory (manual check + annual), BACP Register. All run nightly batch; suspensions trigger 48h unlist SLA.

What this deliberately isn't: not a booking platform (we deep-link to NHS e-Referral or practitioner's own booking) · not rated or reviewed by users (no star ratings on practitioners — avoids gaming + protects clinicians) · not a marketing channel (no paid listings, ever) · not a cultural stereotype machine (no "British" wellness clichés, no clichéd regional humour, no tea-and-biscuits as medicine).

Dependency gate: ships only after (a) Lifestyle v2 live (which hosts Foods-by-phase tab), (b) Jess v2 voice brief signed off (so regional variants inherit same tone guardrails), (c) NHS Digital partner-agreement for deep-links to e-Referral confirmed — this is the long-lead item.
