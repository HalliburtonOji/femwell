The conversion thesis

Plus has been theoretical because the unlocked features were nice, not needed. Atelier Reading is monthly content — consumed, not converted-on. Programs Pro is a niche pull (PCOS/PMDD/Peri/Meno specifically). Cycle Settings advanced is for irregular cycles. None of these create a sharp moment-of-need.

Doctor-Ready Diary changes the curve. It's a transactional artefact a user grabs before a specific GP appointment. The "moment of need" is real (perimenopause user, GP visit in 8 days). NICE-NG23 alignment makes it credible. £4.99/mo is a near-trivial decision; £8.99 is not.

Pricing — single tier at £4.99/mo · £39/yr

TierPricePulls

or £39/yr		Doctor-Ready Diary full export (the headline pull) · Atelier Reading monthly letter · Programs Pro pathways · Advanced cycle settings · TTC Mode + Skin & Hair + Sessions Garden (as they ship).

Why not two tiers? Every tier doubles the page complexity and halves the conversion. A buyer values pricing clarity at acquisition; tier proliferation signals "they don't know who they're selling to." Single tier with one clear price is the textbook play.
