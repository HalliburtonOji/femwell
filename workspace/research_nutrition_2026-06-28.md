## TOP 10 TAKEAWAYS FOR FEMWELL

1. **"Cycle syncing your diet" is popular-practice, NOT evidence-based as a rigid protocol — but "cycle *awareness*" is defensible.** What's real: luteal-phase appetite rises (~159–529 kcal/day), iron matters around bleeding, foundational nutrition beats phase-timed rules. Position FemWell as "cycle-aware, not cycle-rules" — a warm, non-prescriptive lens, never a 28-day meal-plan straitjacket (source: https://thirdsister.co.uk/diet-for-hormones-evidence-based-guide/).

2. **Iron is THE women's-nutrition headline in the UK.** ~19% of UK women of reproductive age are anaemic; ~1 in 3 have absolute iron deficiency; ~24% of teen girls are iron-deficient. This is the single biggest evidence-based, UK-specific, life-stage-spanning nutrition story FemWell can own — from teen to perimenopause (source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2025.1679989/full).

3. **Protein matters most in perimenopause/menopause — but the wider "protein obsession" is over-hyped.** Real: 1.0–1.2 g/kg (up to ~1.2–1.6 g/kg for active/older/weight-loss) supports muscle + satiety as oestrogen falls. Hype: protein-maxxing every meal crowds out fibre. Frame protein as "enough, at each meal," not a cult (sources: https://elektrahealth.com/blog/protein-during-menopause/, https://mcpress.mayoclinic.org/nutrition-fitness/debunking-the-protein-propaganda-how-much-is-too-much/).

4. **Anchor every clinical claim to NHS / BDA / British Menopause Society — UK trust signals.** The BDA's menopause tips and NHS pregnancy-supplement pages give FemWell credible, free, citable backbone copy. Use them; don't invent (sources: https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/top-ten-nutrition-tips-for-the-menopause.html, https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/pregnancy-vitamins-and-supplements/).

5. **Lead with JOY, not compliance.** Eating together triggers the endorphin system and is robustly linked to wellbeing; eating alone correlates with poorer mental/physical health. A "what are you eating tonight?" community room is as on-brand and evidence-justified as any micronutrient card (source: https://www.socialconnectionguidelines.org/en/evidence-briefs/should-we-make-eating-together-a-priority).

6. **Cook videos belong INSIDE cards — and YouTube is the only platform that's clean to embed.** YouTube iframe API: embedding allowed, well-documented, works in a card. BUT you may NOT overlay/cover the player or modify it (so card chrome must sit *around*, not *over* it). TikTok needs their `embed.js`; Instagram oEmbed now requires a registered Meta app + lost author/thumbnail fields (Nov 2025). Default to **YouTube embeds + self-hosted HLS for original FemWell content** (sources: https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/required-minimum-functionality, https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/oembed/).

7. **Gut–brain / food–mood is real but modest — sell it gently.** RCTs show diet/fermented-food shifts can improve mild anxiety/low mood via the microbiota–gut–brain axis; probiotic *supplements* are inconsistent. "30 plants a week" + fermented foods is a joyful, evidence-justified hook — not a cure claim (sources: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41026445/, https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/menopause-diet.html).

8. **Name the myths to AVOID, in-app, as a trust feature.** "Cortisol/adrenal cocktails," "seed oils are toxic," "adrenal fatigue" are influencer myths with no evidence. A warm "myth vs fact" card series turns FemWell into the trusted friend who debunks gently (sources: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/cortisol-cocktails-stress-weight-loss-experts/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_oil_misinformation).

9. **The GLP-1 era is a live, unmet need — handle with care.** Women on Wegovy/Ozempic need protein + resistance training to avoid muscle/nutrient loss. FemWell can offer warm, non-judgemental "eating well on GLP-1" support WITHOUT moralising — a real gap (sources: https://health.usnews.com/best-diet/medication/articles/glp-1-muscle-loss-how-to-prevent-muscle-wasting-on-wegovy-and-other-glp-1s, https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/new-dietary-guidelines-weight-loss-drugs).

10. **The UK menopause-nutrition market is booming and partly predatory (£16bn, "Davina effect").** Opportunity: be the credible-but-warm counterweight to "rogue brands making spurious claims" — phytoestrogens framed honestly (works for *some*, takes 2–3 months), not as miracle (sources: https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/analysis-and-features/inside-the-16bn-menopause-market-money-spinner-or-scam/695454.article, https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/menopause-diet.html).

## SECTION 1 — Women's nutrition across the lifespan

### Cycle / menstrual-phase eating
- **[POPULAR-PRACTICE / WEAK]** Rigid cycle-syncing diets: "there's very limited evidence supporting rigid cycle syncing protocols" — hormonal swings differ between women *and between cycles in the same woman* (source: https://thirdsister.co.uk/diet-for-hormones-evidence-based-guide/).
- **[EVIDENCE-BASED]** Luteal-phase appetite/cravings: women eat ~159–529 kcal/day more mid-luteal vs early-follicular, driven by progesterone (source: https://thirdsister.co.uk/diet-for-hormones-evidence-based-guide/).
- **[EVIDENCE-BASED]** Iron around menstruation: periods are the most common cause of iron-deficiency anaemia in women of reproductive age (source: https://www.nhsinform.scot/illnesses-and-conditions/nutritional/iron-deficiency-anaemia/).
- **Recommendation for FemWell:** brand it "cycle-aware, not cycle-rules." A gentle luteal "you might be hungrier — that's biology, eat" note is warm AND accurate; a prescriptive 4-phase meal plan is not.

### Key micronutrients — who needs more, when (UK NDNS data)
- **[EVIDENCE-BASED]** UK women fall short on **calcium, iron, magnesium, potassium, selenium, iodine and copper**; magnesium, iodine and selenium intakes have fallen over the last decade (source: https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=146610).
- **Iron:** ~19.2% of UK reproductive-age women anaemic; ~31.6% have absolute iron deficiency; ~24% of teen girls iron-deficient (sources: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2025.1679989/full, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12078874/).
- **Folate:** 400µg pre-conception → first 12 weeks; 5mg if higher-risk (diabetes, NTD history, epilepsy/HIV meds) (source: https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/pregnancy-vitamins-and-supplements/).
- **Vitamin D:** NHS advises 10µg/day Oct–Mar for most; year-round for over-65s, darker skin, covered/indoors; pregnancy 10µg/day (cap 100µg) (sources: https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/pregnancy-vitamins-and-supplements/, https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/menopause-diet.html).
- **Calcium:** in the UK one-quarter of teen girls don't meet minimum calcium needs — adolescence is peak-bone-mass window (source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18494428/). BDA menopause guidance: a calcium source ~3×/day (source: https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/top-ten-nutrition-tips-for-the-menopause.html).
- **B12 + iodine (vegans):** vegan women have markedly lower iodine (~24µg) and B12; B12 must come from fortified foods/supplements; iodine is a real vegan vulnerability (sources: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7251157/, https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/endocrinology/articles/10.3389/fendo.2025.1537208/full).
- **Omega-3:** BDA recommends oily fish 1–2×/week for heart + mood (source: https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/top-ten-nutrition-tips-for-the-menopause.html).

### Protein discourse (how much is real)
- **[MIXED, leans positive]** Menopause: ~1.0–1.2 g/kg/day, up to ~1.2–1.6 g/kg for active/older/weight-loss; supports lean muscle + satiety as oestrogen declines — but underlying evidence is "limited and not of high quality" (sources: https://elektrahealth.com/blog/protein-during-menopause/, https://www.mdpi.com/2673-9488/4/3/16).
- **[POPULAR-PRACTICE / OVER-HYPED]** The general "protein obsession": ~25–35g/meal meets most needs; over-focusing crowds out fibre (sources: https://www.today.com/health/diet-fitness/too-much-protein-habit-stop-dietitian-tip-rcna341461, https://mcpress.mayoclinic.org/nutrition-fitness/debunking-the-protein-propaganda-how-much-is-too-much/).

## SECTION 2 — Life-stage specifics

- **PCOS [EVIDENCE-BASED]:** low-GI eating → less insulin resistance, more regular cycles; **inositol** now endorsed in the 2023 International Evidence-Based PCOS Guidelines (improves ovulation, cycle regularity, glycaemia; metabolic/hormonal certainty lower) (sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11698792/, https://academic.oup.com/jcem/article/109/6/1630/7504796).
- **PMS/PMDD [EVIDENCE-BASED, moderate]:** 1200mg calcium cut PMDD severity ~48% in trial; magnesium (often low in PMS) + B6 beats magnesium alone; RCOG lists B6 as first-line despite low-grade evidence (sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4794546/, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3208934/, https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2023.1079417/full).
- **Endometriosis [MIXED/EMERGING]:** Mediterranean/anti-inflammatory diets associated with less pain + better GI symptoms; high red/processed meat associated with higher risk; high caffeine (>300mg/day) + butter associated with higher risk; vitamins C/E/D, Mg, omega-3, curcumin "promising but variable" (source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12787854/).
- **Pre-conception/TTC [EVIDENCE-BASED]:** 400µg folic acid before conception; vitamin D 10µg; avoid vitamin A (source: https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/pregnancy-vitamins-and-supplements/).
- **Pregnancy by trimester [EVIDENCE-BASED, NHS]:** folic acid through wk 12; vitamin D throughout; iron checked at booking + ~28 weeks, supplemented if anaemic; no cod liver oil/retinol; Healthy Start free vitamins for eligible families (source: https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/pregnancy-vitamins-and-supplements/).
- **Perimenopause/menopause [EVIDENCE-BASED, BDA]** — backbone copy from BDA Top-Ten:
  - Strength exercise ≥2×/week + portion awareness (weight gain is largely *ageing*, not menopause per se).
  - Regular meals, especially breakfast — eases mood swings/fatigue.
  - Fibre: choose ≥3g/100g (source) or 6g/100g (high-fibre).
  - Calcium source ~3×/day + 10µg vitamin D daily.
  - Oily fish 1–2×/week (omega-3).
  - Phytoestrogens (soya/linseed) 2–3×/day for ~3 months — *may* ease flushes in some.
  - Identify flush triggers: caffeine, alcohol, spicy foods, hot drinks.
  - 5-a-day; cholesterol check (sources: https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/top-ten-nutrition-tips-for-the-menopause.html, https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/menopause-diet.html).
- **Menopause-nutrition market context:** ~£16bn market, "Davina effect" boom, with concern about "rogue brands making spurious claims" — FemWell's wedge is *credible warmth* (sources: https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/analysis-and-features/inside-the-16bn-menopause-market-money-spinner-or-scam/695454.article, https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13675494241287931).

## SECTION 3 — Food ↔ mood / energy / gut

- **[MIXED/EMERGING, leans positive]** Gut–brain axis: mounting RCT data that diet change can improve mental-health outcomes by shifting gut microbes; effects clearest for *mild* anxiety/low mood (source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41026445/).
- **[MIXED]** Fermented foods inversely associated with social anxiety in observational data; probiotic *supplements* for mood remain inconsistent (sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6469458/, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-nutrition-society/article/fibre-fermented-foods-differential-effects-on-the-microbiotagutbrain-axis/1FCFCF34665A6E10E34928301CA9EDE3).
- **[EVIDENCE-BASED]** Fibre diversity ("30 plants/week") supports microbiome diversity — BDA echoes this in menopause guidance (source: https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/menopause-diet.html).
- **Solid vs hyped:** SOLID — fibre diversity, regular meals for steady energy/mood, fermented foods as a gentle adjunct. HYPED — probiotic supplements as mood "cures," any single-food fix.

## SECTION 4 — Online discourse 2024–2026 (what's resonating / what to avoid)

- **Intuitive eating / anti-diet [EVIDENCE-BASED psychologically]:** linked to better body image, self-esteem, self-compassion, less binge eating + depression/anxiety. CAUTION: anti-diet influencers over-extend it into un-evidenced claims (sources: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12430477/, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12336771/).
- **Protein trend [OVER-HYPED]:** real for older/active women; cultishness crowds out fibre (source: https://mcpress.mayoclinic.org/nutrition-fitness/debunking-the-protein-propaganda-how-much-is-too-much/).
- **GLP-1 era (Ozempic/Wegovy) [LIVE NEED]:** appetite over-suppression → risk of muscle loss, nutrient deficiency, constipation, dehydration; mitigate with adequate protein + resistance training 2–3×/week; registered-dietitian support recommended. Big, under-served, sensitive space (sources: https://health.usnews.com/best-diet/medication/articles/glp-1-muscle-loss-how-to-prevent-muscle-wasting-on-wegovy-and-other-glp-1s, https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/new-dietary-guidelines-weight-loss-drugs).
- **Fibermaxxing [EVIDENCE-BASED if gradual]:** Gen-Z-led; good from whole foods; ramp over weeks, not days; "fibre diversity" is the 2026 evolution (sources: https://mcpress.mayoclinic.org/nutrition-fitness/fibermaxxing-is-this-tiktok-trend-good-for-you/, https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/10/21/fibermaxxing-fades-as-fiber-diversity-dominates-2026-trends/).
- **MYTHS TO AVOID, name them in-app:**
  - "Cortisol/adrenal cocktails" + "adrenal fatigue" — no evidence; influencers monetise vague "hormone imbalance" (source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/cortisol-cocktails-stress-weight-loss-experts/).
  - "Seed oils are toxic" — debunked; no consistent inflammation/disease signal; omega-6/linoleic acid linked to *lower* CV risk (sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_oil_misinformation, https://www.health.harvard.edu/heart-health/seeding-doubt-the-truth-about-cooking-oils).
- **Positioning:** credible-but-warm "smart friend." Cite NHS/BDA, flag what's emerging vs hyped, and run a gentle "myth vs fact" card series as a *trust* feature, not a lecture.

## SECTION 5 — FUN & FOOD (critical — weight this heavily)

- **[EVIDENCE-BASED]** Eating together triggers the brain's endorphin system + supports social bonding; eating alone correlates with poorer mental + physical health and loneliness (sources: https://www.socialconnectionguidelines.org/en/evidence-briefs/should-we-make-eating-together-a-priority, https://www.nationalgeographic.com/health/article/communal-dining-loneliness-epidemic).
- **[EVIDENCE-BASED]** Commensality builds identity + community; "food and sharing food are very important elements in building identities, both as individuals and as members of communities" (source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8295993/).
- **[EVIDENCE-BASED]** Shared meals nudge healthier eating + improve mental health; intergenerational eating builds cross-generation understanding (sources: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8345784/, https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13607863.2025.2562138).
- **FemWell hooks (all defensible):** "What are you eating tonight?" community thread; comfort-food + cultural-dish swaps; "vent about dinner"; seasonal/celebratory eating (Sunday roast, Eid, Diwali, Christmas); cooking-as-creativity; a low-stakes "win" (one new plant this week). Frame food as pleasure + connection, never compliance — this is on-brand AND evidence-supported wellbeing.

## SECTION 6 — Cook / recipe VIDEOS (landscape + the critical embed feasibility)

### Why short cook video works
- Short-form is the highest-reach format; algorithms surface to non-followers at zero cost (source: https://www.marketeze.ai/blog/food-creators-guide-to-viral-hooks-make-mouths-water-in-3-seconds).
- Food triggers a dopamine response on sight; viewers decide subconsciously within ~0.3s, consciously by ~3s → hook in first seconds, tease the finished dish (source: https://www.marketeze.ai/blog/food-creators-guide-to-viral-hooks-make-mouths-water-in-3-seconds).
- Sweet spot 15–60s; "5-minute" simple/satisfying tutorials win; TikTok = trend/authentic, Reels = polished, Shorts = how-to (source: https://bluebearcreative.co/blog/how-to-create-viral-tiktok-food-recipe-videos-a-guide/).

### Embed feasibility — technical + legal (the load-bearing part)

**YouTube — RECOMMENDED for third-party content.**
- iframe Player API: embedding is the documented standard; params include `playsinline`, `autoplay`, `mute`, `controls`, `enablejsapi`; min viewport 200×200 (rec 480×270) (source: https://developers.google.com/youtube/iframe_api_reference).
- **HARD CONSTRAINT:** "You must not display overlays, frames, or other visual elements *in front of* any part of a YouTube embedded player, including player controls," and "must not make changes to the YouTube player not described by the API." Cards are fine *around* the player; nothing may cover it. Only one player autoplays per page; in-app must use WKWebView (iOS) / WebView (Android) with correct HTTP Referer (source: https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/required-minimum-functionality).
- **UX implication for FemWell cards:** the inline player must be a clean rectangle with card chrome (hook line, action, attribution) ABOVE/BELOW it — never a tap-overlay covering the video. Autoplay muted + `playsinline` for a small card; tap to unmute/expand.

**TikTok — possible but heavier.**
- Two paths: manual Share→Embed, or oEmbed (`GET /oembed?url=`). Output is a `<blockquote class="tiktok-embed">` that REQUIRES loading `https://www.tiktok.com/embed.js` to render the real player; otherwise users get a fallback. Attribution (creator, sound) is auto-displayed; removed videos disappear (source: https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/embed-videos/).
- Implication: third-party script dependency + less control over card sizing → secondary, not primary.

**Instagram Reels — most restricted, avoid as primary.**
- Old oEmbed endpoints removed April 2025; must use Meta **oEmbed Read**, which needs a registered Facebook app, app ID/secret, and oEmbed-Read setup. From 3 Nov 2025, responses dropped `author_name`, `thumbnail_url` etc. Basic Display API ended Dec 2024 (sources: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/oembed/, the deprecation summary at https://sociavault.com/blog/instagram-api-deprecated-alternative-2026).
- Implication: high friction + moving target → not worth it for inline cards now.

**Vimeo / self-hosted HLS — RECOMMENDED for ORIGINAL FemWell content.**
- For FemWell's own recipe videos, self-hosting (HLS / a player like Vimeo or Mux) gives full card control, no third-party ToS overlay rules, no attribution chrome, no "video removed" risk. Best for owned, branded, on-brand cook clips inside the Nutrition/Lifestyle cards.

**Net embed recommendation:** YouTube embed for curated third-party recipes (respect the no-overlay rule); self-hosted HLS/Vimeo for FemWell-original cook clips; treat TikTok/Instagram as share-out links, not inline players.
