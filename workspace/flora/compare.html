Flora engine — before & after

The same three “hero” flowers, same code path (RichBloomV2), rebuilt for recognisability.
Before
Small, flat, one radial silhouette — the “rose” reads as a generic red bloom; peony ≈ camellia ≈ ranunculus. Only the sunflower is recognisable.
After
Bespoke per-species geometry at generous scale: the rose shows layered guard petals + a visible spiral heart; the hibiscus shows 5 broad petals + the staminal column; peony/dahlia/tulip/poppy/lily all carry distinct silhouettes; pale blooms hold their edge; the butterflies are real four-wing creatures.
