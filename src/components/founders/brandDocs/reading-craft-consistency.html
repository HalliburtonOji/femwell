Reading craft

Fixing the book reader showed the articles had it worse. So: audit every reading surface, fix them the same way, and build one foundation so it never drifts again.

The root cause — invisible from the code

Two fonts were secretly 1.5× bigger than they said.

src/index.css remaps both 'Inter' and 'Fraunces' to Cormorant at size-adjust: 150%. So a surface declaring font-size: 16px in 'Inter' actually renders at ~24px — while a surface using the SERIF stack renders at face value. Nothing in the component tells you this.

That single line explains the whole inconsistency: the two reading engines sat at ~29 characters per line (some sizes as low as 22) while every SERIF surface was a healthy ~45–50. It was never a "the articles are a bit off" problem — it was a font remap nobody could see.

The research corrected me twice honest

I was about to fix it the wrong way.

  • "Use max-width: 66ch" — wrong. 1ch is the width of the "0" glyph, ~20–30% wider than an average character. 66ch actually yields ~80–85 characters — over the accessibility cap. (Tailwind's prose ships 65ch, so the reference implementation carries the bug.) The right unit is ex: USWDS — the only design system that tokenises measure — puts 66 characters at 60ex.
  • "Get above 45 CPL" — unreachable, and I said it too confidently last pass. At 390px, 45 characters needs ~16px type and ~16px margins. The honest target on a phone is 38–42, and you must never shrink type to buy characters (cream's readability advantage actually grows as type gets smaller). At 390px the max-width never binds — padding IS measure. So the bug was always structural (double-framing), not typographic.
  • Read-time cuts both ways. Stating a 10-minute cost made fewer people finish. So: "4 min read" proudly; past ~10 min, say nothing.
  • No article progress bar. No evidence they help; across 32 experiments they backfire when early investment is high — a long read is exactly that.
The foundation

One column. It owns measure, padding and leading — nothing else may.

brand/ReadingColumn.jsx is now the only thing allowed to set those on any reading surface. It enforces, structurally rather than by copy discipline:

  • Measure as a token, in ex (60ex ≈ 66 chars) — most systems tokenise size and leading and leave measure to whoever writes the page. That is how six surfaces drift.
  • width: min(measure, 100%) + margin-inline:auto — self-clamping. Never max-width under a padded ancestor.
  • Sole padding owner; type floors 18/16px; leading 1.62.
  • Indent XOR space by variant — article/horoscope/card spaced; fiction/book/dailyStory indented. Never both.
  • Escape hatches on day one (bleed, measure="none") — a component with no legal exit gets forked, and forking is drift.
  • Always the un-adjusted SERIF stack, so it can't fall into the 150% trap.

Flagged for its own component

The story engine — deliberately, not as a sweep.

DailyStoryReader is 2,033 lines and is the engine behind both FictionReader and BookReader — it's three surfaces at once. Its pagination is measured against the live font metrics, so changing the typeface or size forces a pagination re-tune; and it owns 5 text sizes, 3 themes, bookmarks, resume and a page-flip animation. It is already on-brand, so nothing is ugly today — it's the measure (~29 CPL) that needs fixing, and doing that safely means re-tuning the pagination with it. Highest blast radius on the page; it deserves a proper pass, not a craft sweep.

Recommended fix when we do it: move .ds-reader-p off the remapped 'Fraunces' onto the un-adjusted stack, re-baseline the 5 text sizes around an 18px floor, and re-tune the pagination measurement — then verify CPL by counting real characters, not by trusting the unit.

Also found

Dead code, and four buttons that lied.

  • Four no-op buttons ("Mark as read", "Reflect on it", "Save tonight's reading", "Sky diary") only closed their sheet. All four now do the real thing — a button that lies is worse than no button, and this is a trust issue, not a craft one.
  • src/pages/Lifestyle.jsx is dead code — no route maps to it (the live page is LifestyleElite → LifestyleEliteShell). Its ForYou/Browse/Listen/Horoscope tabs are unreachable. Left untouched this pass; worth a deliberate delete so nobody "fixes" a page nobody can open.
