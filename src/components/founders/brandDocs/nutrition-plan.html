The whole table

Nutrition is one of the most load-bearing rooms in a woman's life — and the place a wholesome app can be genuinely useful and joyful. Here's what FemWell's Nutrition does today (honestly), what the evidence says, and the full plan to finish it — including cook videos that play inside our cards. The one-line thesis

A diet app counts calories and shames. A whole-life nutrition room makes eating easier, kinder and more joyful — it logs in seconds, quietly looks after the nutrients that actually matter for her stage, plans real meals with real cooking, and treats food as pleasure, culture and connection — not a test.

The headline moves (this plan)

  • Finish the half-built core: a real meal-plan generator, real recipes (save + "cook what's in" + generate), and the logger's last stubs (photo). These are the visible gaps today.
  • Cook videos INSIDE the cards — the recipe lens becomes a swipeable deck of embedded cook clips (YouTube embed for curated, self-hosted for FemWell-original), playing in-card per the bible.
  • Lead with JOY: a "what's for dinner tonight?" food room, cultural/celebratory dishes, comfort food, cook-along — eating together is evidence-backed wellbeing, not a side feature.
  • Quiet, credible, stage-aware guidance anchored to NHS / BDA — iron as the flagship women's story; "cycle-aware, not cycle-rules"; a warm myth-vs-fact series as a trust feature.

2 · The evidence (cited)

What actually matters for women

Every clinical claim should ride NHS · BDA · British Menopause Society — free, trusted, citable. The brand's edge is being credible AND warm in a market that's often neither. Each row is flagged evidence, popular-practice or myth.

01Iron is the flagship women's story

~19% of UK reproductive-age women are anaemic, ~1 in 3 iron-deficient, ~24% of teen girls deficient — it spans teen → perimenopause (heavier bleeds). This is the single most useful cross-life-stage nutrition story FemWell can own. → Make iron a recurring, gentle thread tied to the cycle (more around bleeding), never alarmist. evidence

Frontiers in Nutrition 2025 (UK iron) · NHS inform

02"Cycle-aware", not "cycle-rules"

Rigid "cycle-syncing your diet" has very limited evidence as a protocol. What IS real: luteal appetite genuinely rises (+159–529 kcal/day), and iron matters around bleeding. → Frame phase guidance as gentle awareness ("your body may want a little more this week"), never a restrictive meal-by-phase rulebook. popular-practice

Third Sister — evidence review

03Protein matters most in peri/menopause

~1.0–1.2 g/kg (up to ~1.6 for active/older women) supports muscle + satiety through menopause — but the wider "protein at every meal / protein-maxxing" trend is over-hyped and crowds out fibre. → Quiet protein nudges for peri/menopause; resist the maxxing aesthetic. leans-evidence, over-hyped

Elektra Health · Mayo Clinic Press

04Life-stage levers that are real

PCOS: low-GI eating + inositol (2023 international guideline). PMS/PMDD: calcium ~1200mg cut PMDD symptoms ~48%; magnesium + B6 help PMS. Pregnancy/TTC: 400µg folic acid pre-conception→wk12, 10µg vitamin D, avoid retinol/liver (NHS). Menopause: the BDA "top ten" backbone — calcium 3×/day + D, oily fish, fibre, strength 2×/wk, flush triggers. evidence

PCOS guideline 2023 · Calcium/PMDD · NHS pregnancy · BDA menopause

05The GLP-1 era is a live, unmet need

Women on Wegovy/Ozempic need protein + resistance support to avoid muscle/nutrient loss — and want non-judgemental help, which almost nothing offers warmly. → A gentle, opt-in "eating well on a GLP-1" thread is a real differentiator. emerging need

US News — GLP-1 & muscle

06Food ↔ mood / gut is real but modest

Diet + fermented foods can lift mild mood; "30 plants a week" supports microbiome diversity. Solid and joyful as a hook — not a cure. → "30 plants" as a playful, optional game; never medicalise. real-but-modest

4 · The load-bearing question

Cook videos inside our cards — yes, here's how

You asked whether we can embed cook videos inside the sliding cards. Yes — and the app is already capable. The Card system has InlineVideo/InlineAudio (Card.jsx), a YouTube/Vimeo iframe player (WorkoutPlayer.jsx), and there's no Content-Security-Policy blocking third-party iframes. The constraints are about which platform and how, not whether.

SourceEmbed in a card?How / caveat
YouTuberecommended (curated)Official iframe API; playsinline + muted autoplay. Hard rule: never put an overlay ON TOP of the player or its controls — chrome goes above/below only.
Self-hosted / HLSrecommended (originals)Full card control, no ToS chrome, no "video removed" risk — best for FemWell-original cook clips. (Adds an hls.js dependency.)
VimeoyesMore player control than YouTube; smaller library. Already supported in WorkoutPlayer.
TikToksecondaryoEmbed blockquote + embed.js; can't theme; vanishes if creator deletes. Fine as an occasional embed, not the backbone.
Instagram Reelslink-out onlyoEmbed now needs an app-reviewed Meta app and (from Nov 2025) drops thumbnail/author data — not worth inlining. Link out.

How it lives in the card language

A recipe becomes a two-half stacked card: the top half a horizontal deck of embedded cook-clip cards (swipe between "the 60-sec version", "the full method", "a variation"); the bottom half the ingredients → "add to shopping" → "log it when you've eaten". Inline play in the card; a "cook full-screen" CTA deep-links the whole recipe — exactly the bible's inline-media rule (§6.7: "inline media plays IN the card; the consume CTA deep-links the exact item full-screen").

7 · Honest build reality

The one real decision: how does "generate" work?

Most of this rides existing entities. But meal-plan + recipe generation needs compute — and the standing rule is no new base44 function. So the key decision to make before building:

  • Option A — template/rules generator (no AI, no function). A curated, stage-aware recipe/meal library + smart filtering (diet, time, what's-in). Ships entirely client-side, fully within the rule. Less "magic", very reliable. safest
  • Option B — route through the existing Jess agent. Jess is already a base44 agent (not a new function) — meal/recipe generation could be a Jess capability. Needs verifying she can return structured recipes. needs a spike
  • Option C — a new AI function. Cleanest "generate anything" UX, but it's the one thing the rule caps — would need your explicit OK. your call

My recommendation: A for v1 (a beautiful curated library + filters covers 90% of the value with zero rule-risk), with a B spike to see if Jess can do bespoke generation later. New small entities (Pantry; saved-recipe writes) are additive, no new function. Everything else (logging, macros, shopping, targets, insights) is already real.
