An actual arcade

Fair feedback: the Games Room was thin — a couple of health-ish prompts, no substance. This is the rethink: real games women actually enjoy — a daily word puzzle, trivia, Ludo, Whot, connect‑4, hangman, would‑you‑rather — with proper how‑to, warm framing, and reasons to come back. Not health‑gated. Just fun, together, safe. The full menu, the mechanics, and an honest feasible‑now vs needs‑sign‑off split — then the strongest real set I can build now.

The one-line thesis

A Games Room earns a daily visit when there's a fresh thing every day (the Daily), something to beat right now (play vs the app), a turn waiting for you (play a real woman), and the room to join (party rounds) — all with zero ranking-shame (celebrate playing, aggregate the room, never a leaderboard). The nudges write themselves: "today's word is up", "your turn in Ludo".

1 · The menu

Five shelves of real games

A · The Daily — one fresh thing every day (solo, but shared)

The daily‑return engine. Same puzzle for everyone each day; play solo; share a spoiler‑safe result; see how the room did (aggregate, no ranking).

  • The Daily Word — a Wordle‑style 5‑letter guess in 6 tries, on‑screen keyboard, share your colour‑block result. The flagship.
  • Daily Trivia — 5 fun general‑knowledge / pop‑culture questions, instant right/wrong, a warm score, "how the room did".
  • Daily Hangman — guess the day's word letter by letter.
  • Daily Number — a tiny logic/number puzzle (guess‑the‑number, a mini sudoku‑lite).

B · Play vs the app — instant, solo, satisfying

No waiting, no opponent needed — a real game against a decent computer, any time.

  • Tic‑tac‑toe (smart‑but‑beatable AI) · Connect‑4 · Dots‑and‑boxes · Hangman (endless words) · a solitaire‑style cosy card game.

C · Play a real woman — async turn‑based (the social heart)

Take a turn, put your phone down, come back when it's your move. Anonymous (botanical alias), no chat required. "Your turn" nudges bring her back.

  • Ludo (2–4) · Whot (the card game) · Connect‑4 · Tic‑tac‑toe · Dots‑and‑boxes · Word duel (take turns building words).
  • Play with the room (matched to any woman), with a circle, or invite a friend (a share link into an existing conversation).

D · Play with the room — party rounds (async, aggregate reveal)

The existing engine, deepened. One prompt, everyone answers, Jess reveals the warm whole.

  • Would‑you‑rather · This‑or‑That · Trivia night · Categories · Story chain · Caption this · Two truths · Hot takes.

E · Cozy solo — wind‑down

Word‑search · flora colour‑by‑number · a gentle memory/match game. No timer, no pressure.

2 · What makes it stick

Mechanics — explanation, nudges, "we" not "who won"

Proper onboarding per game

Every game opens with a one‑card how‑to (2–3 lines + an example), a warm framing ("no timer, no losers"), and a big "Play" button. No one should ever stare at a board wondering what to do.

Reasons to come back (nudges)

"Today's word is up" · "Your turn in Ludo with Wild Poppy" · "Trivia night starts this evening" · a gentle "you're on a 4‑day streak with the Daily" (private, for you — never public).

Aggregate, never a leaderboard

Celebrate playing: "you solved it in 4 · the room's average today was 4.2" · "68% of the room picked cosy night in". Never a ranked list of people. A missed day never breaks anything shameful.

Share into a room, softly

One tap to drop your result (the spoiler‑safe blocks, your trivia score) into the Lighter Side — a conversation starter, never a brag.

Featured & the Together rhythm

A game of the day + a game of the week tied to the Together "this week" moment, so the Games Room has a heartbeat.

3 · Safe & kind

Safety — fun without the sharp edges

  • Anonymous by default — botanical alias; you never need a real name to play.
  • No ranking‑shame — no public leaderboards, no "you lost" framing, no counts that shame a lurker.
  • Moderation on any user text — caption/story‑chain/word entries go through the same publish‑then‑screen + crisis‑check as posts; report/block in the "…" menu.
  • Opt‑out of multiplayer — solo + vs‑app options for anyone who doesn't want to play a stranger; block a match anytime.
  • No money, no gambling — purely for joy.
4 · The honest split

Feasible now vs needs your sign-off

Game / capabilityClass
The Daily Word (Wordle‑style, deterministic daily word, share blocks)NOW — client-side, no backend
Daily Trivia (client question bank, score, share)NOW
Play vs the app — Tic‑tac‑toe, Connect‑4, Dots‑and‑boxes, Hangman (AI opponent)NOW

Private "your streak" + aggregate "how the room did"NOW (device‑local streak; light aggregate)
Play a REAL woman — async turn‑based (Tic‑tac‑toe/Connect‑4/Dots‑&‑boxes vs a person)NEEDS SIGN‑OFF — new GameMatch entity + a move/matchmaking function
Ludo · Whot (multi‑player board/card, dealer/state)BIGGER BUILD + GameMatch entity/fn (do after the 2‑player base proves out)
Live / real‑time rounds (everyone at once)NEEDS REAL‑TIME INFRA (websockets/polling fn) — defer

Why the split: anything you play against a real woman needs shared, turn‑based state → a GameMatch entity (board · players' aliases · whose turn · status) + an anonymous move function (writes are service‑role + moderated, like posts). That's one entity + one function — lean, but it's the line that needs your OK. Everything solo/daily/vs‑app/with‑the‑room is buildable now with zero new backend.

5 · The recommendation + what shipped

Build the "now" arcade first — real substance, zero backend

Recommended first build (shipped this pass): a proper arcade home + these real games, all client‑side:

  • The Daily Word — full Wordle‑style game (deterministic daily answer, 6 guesses, on‑screen keyboard, win/lose, private streak, share your result blocks). The flagship daily‑return hook.
  • Daily Trivia — 5 rotating fun questions, instant feedback + a warm score, share it.
  • Tic‑tac‑toe vs the app — a real smart‑but‑beatable opponent, instant play.
  • Hangman — guess the word, endless bank.
  • With the room — the party prompt games kept, framed as one shelf.
  • Proper how‑to on each, warm framing, private streaks, "share into the Lighter Side", on‑brand canonical page + back‑nav.

Then, on your sign‑off: the GameMatch entity + move function unlocks play‑a‑real‑woman (Connect‑4 / Tic‑tac‑toe / Dots‑&‑boxes async), and after that the bigger Ludo / Whot builds. Real‑time is a later, separate call.

The point: this is the arcade you can open every day and actually enjoy — before we even touch multiplayer. Then multiplayer makes it social.
