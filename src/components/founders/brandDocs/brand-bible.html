One · The soul Agreed

Your life, in bloom.

A woman's life is a garden, not a chart.

It has seasons (the cycle), long arcs (life-stages, teen-daisy → elder-magnolia), and many beds, not one — career, friendship, love, money, rest, joy, identity. Health is one room, not the house. The flora isn't decoration; it's a language — every flower means something true, and means the same thing everywhere it appears.

The lane we own: femtech runs from pink straight into clinical neutrality (Clue, Elvie). FemWell runs the other way — into editorial warmth: cream, gold and crimson; a serif voice; a living botanical world. Beautiful where they're sterile. Breadth of life carries the femininity, not pink.

Two · Voice & tone Agreed

A warm, funny smart-friend with soul.

What you asked for, in your words: language that's less generic, more funny, nurturing, with soul — never cold, never clinical, never a scoreboard, never guilt. UK English. No emoji. Lighthearted by default; let life-stage gently tint the tone, never dominate it.

Do
“Rough night? Let's keep today gentle — three small things that tend to help are below. No pressure.”
Warm, specific, optional. A friend, not a chart.
Don't
“You missed your hydration goal 3 days in a row. Streak lost.”
Scoreboard, guilt, clinical. Off-brand.

The principles

Notice, don't cheerleadobserve something true about today — “rough night?” beats “you've got this!”
Specific over sentimentend on a small real thing: “send the text,” not “good things await.”
Funny, warmlydry, UK, a wink in small places (a card footer, an empty state) — never zany, never every screen.
Soul, not saccharineif a line would fit unedited on a supermarket card, cut it.
Never punish a gapa hard stretch is weather, not a failure. Gentle re-entry, always.
A few in-voice lines (agreed register)

“Quiet morning. The garden's resting and, honestly, so should you. Today's a tea-and-low-lights kind of day.”

“Nothing here yet — and that's allowed. Plant the first line whenever you like; the garden's patient.”

“There you are. No ledger here, no streak to mourn — just, welcome back. I left the kettle on.”

You also liked some omen-flavoured lines (“a bird's on your plant — good news might be coming,” “the stars are singing…”). Those are lovely and point at the soul you want — but a whole systematic omen/almanac voice (the heavier “a little mystic” register) is written up in Proposed for your explicit yes, not assumed here.

The rules that end the “fonts are a mess” problem: Ephesis = short titles only (never all-caps/long). Cormorant = every heading + all reading. System sans = chrome only. Never set Inter/Fraunces expecting a sans (they're remapped to Cormorant). Every node maps to a role — no free font sizes; allowed sizes 12·13·14·15·16·17·18·20 + two clamps; nothing readable below 12px, body ≥16.

Five · The flora system Agreed

A real garden — 64 flowers, each itself.

The first rule: a rose reads as a rose.

The recognisability standard

Silhouette first, colour second — bespoke geometry per species, at generous scale, with tonal depth (lit tip → shadowed throat), fine edge linework, and a luminance fix so pale blooms hold their edge on cream. The three heroes carry signatures: rose = a visible spiral heart; sunflower = ray florets + a phyllotaxis seed-disc; hibiscus = five broad petals + the projecting staminal column.

The botanical library — 64 named flowers (+ foliage)

This is what “variety” means: a genuine library, not a handful. floraLibrary.jsx · <SpeciesBloom name size/>. Every page/section draws a different signature species from it — never the same three flowers everywhere.

Foliage & greenery

Lifecycle — the stage is the meaning

The same flower at a different stage says where she is, without a word: bud → bloom → seed (the rose hip) → rest (the bare cane, holding next year's bloom). Rest is a stage, not a failure — we never draw “nothing,” we draw dormancy with a bud on it.

Meaning, fingerprint & page character

Flowers carry documented floriography across the whole of life (career = gladiolus, friendship = sunflower+daisy, grief = forget-me-not, eldership = magnolia…); colour changes the meaning. Each woman's garden is a deterministic fingerprint — the same on every device, recognisably hers, grown from who she is and what she tends. Each surface gets a signature species + a palette lean (Journal = willow + gold; Community = clover + sage; Nutrition = herbs + blush-green), all from the one dictionary.

Six · Cards & the quick-action popup Agreed

Cards are the language.

Never hand-roll a <div> card — import the typed variants (brand/Card.jsx). Each carries an icon + eyebrow + meaning-bloom, a hook (the one line that earns it) + a line (or a real snippet / inline player), and an inline action. No empty containers. Sized to the Today “across your day” card.

The quick-action popup — “do it right here”

When she taps a doable micro-task on a page — read today's chapter · answer the Question of the Day · leave a line · log breakfast/mood · tick a ritual · set today's intention — it opens a quick, lightweight popup so she DOES it in place, then it ticks. Not a link-out (don't navigate away for a 10-second task); not a bare checkbox (don't let her tick what she hasn't done).

ActionWhat opens in the popup
Read today's chapterthe chapter (or its opening + “read on”) inline; ticks “read”
Question of the Daythe question + a quick input (text / choice chips) → ticks
Leave a linea mini-composer (textarea + tag) → saves & ticks
Log a meal / moodrecent + searchable chips, or a quick mood selector → ticks
Tick a rituala confirm + optional note → ticks

Quick-popup for short do tasks; the full-screen deep-link (the exact item) for long-form consume (a book, a programme session). On completion the source item ticks gently; writes ride existing actions — no new function.

Seven · Page structure Agreed

The signature top.

Every primary page opens the same way, then varies below: 1 · a flora hero (a large brand flower in a decorative botanical ring + the carved heart + an Ephesis title + a warm line) → 2 · one summary card (a signal-driven “what's here today,” never hollow) → 3 · page-specific rich cards. All on PAPER_BG (the paper-grain background), never a flat fill. Multi-section pages get the central “Jump to” switcher.

Eight · Components, motion & nav Agreed

The fittings.

The carved crimson heart is the brand mark — always #BC2E27, exactly one per surface. Botanical motifs are hairline, stroke-only line-art, one per fold, never behind text. Buttons/chips/sheets are accent-driven, radius-999 chips, paperHi bottom-sheets over a warm scrim (with nav clearance). One unified floating glass-capsule bottom nav at every viewport; Jess is a crimson bloom in the row. Icons: Lucide / inline SVG only — never emoji.

Motion = breath, not bounce. Transform/opacity only (GPU); micro 120ms · standard 200ms · sheets 300ms; no bounce anywhere; high-frequency taps stay ≤120ms or un-animated; prefers-reduced-motion gated on every animated node.

Nine · Before you build Agreed

The 60-second gate.

  1. Type — every node maps to a role; nothing below 12px.
  2. Colour — only the tokens; body text near-black only.
  3. Heart — exactly one carved crimson heart per surface.
  4. Flora — recognisable species (a rose reads as a rose), generous scale, variety visible; pull from the 64-flower library.
  5. Cards — from brand/Card.jsx, never empty; short do = quick-action popup, long consume = exact-item deep-link.
  6. Page — flora hero → one summary card → rich content, all on PAPER_BG.
  7. Always — cream/ink world, Ephesis + Cormorant + system sans, Lucide/SVG, no emoji, UK English, no scoreboards/guilt.

If a screen reads as plain, monochrome or sparse, it's off-brand — push it richer. Restraint here means cohesion and craft, not emptiness.

P1 · The omen system Proposed

Creatures and plants that carry a gentle, hopeful omen tied to her day — your “a bird's on your plant” idea, made a system. A rotating, signal-keyed flower/bouquet heads each section and is tappable → a little meaning reveal (the flower's meaning + a “they say…” line + why it's here for her today). Safety rails: ration it (one a day, not every screen), never resurface a hard memory unprompted, hope-only readings.

Example omen line

“There's a robin on your snowdrop this morning. They say a little bird means news on its way — and after the week you've had, the good kind's overdue. Keep your phone close.”

P2 · The “a little mystic” voice intensity Proposed

A heavier, almanac/folklore register on top of the agreed warm-funny voice — the “the stars are singing…”, “they say…”, gardener's-lore tone. You questioned the “a little bit witch” framing, fairly — so it sits here, dialled to taste, until you choose how mystic (my suggestion: gentle, always earthed by a domestic wink — “…wash something off today: a worry, a grudge, the kettle”).

P3 · Craft that carries meaning Proposed

Health “letters” sealed with a wax rose; a monthly “letter from your body”; vines that grow with progress instead of a bar; saved things pressed like flowers in a book (“on this day last year…”). Format as feeling.

P4 · The brand archetype Proposed

A formal personality anchor — Caregiver core, tinted Sage, a touch of the Innocent's joy (“a warm friend who knows things and keeps it joyful”). Useful as an internal north star for tone decisions; not user-facing. Yours to adopt or leave.
