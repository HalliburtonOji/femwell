FemWell · Community · FEATURE #2 of 6 · Together / Events · brainstorm + build

Nobody walks in alone

Real things to do — near you and online — that women can go to together. Find one, say you're going, bring a friend. And because in-person meetups are the part where safety really matters, the safety model is built into how it works, not bolted on. This is the deep-brainstorm and what's now built into the redesign demo, grounded in cited research on how women's meetup apps actually keep people safe.

The one-line thesis

The loneliest group in the UK is young women — and the thing that helps isn't another feed, it's showing up somewhere with people. So we surface real events with ticket links (we never take payment), let a woman say "I'm going" (kept private), help her bring a friend, and wrap it all in a safety model that treats venue-level-only location, tell-a-friend and trust signals as non-negotiable defaults.

1 · The model

What "Together / Events" is

Two ways in — in person and online — across the whole of life, not just health: socials, walks, gigs & comedy, career coffees, fashion swaps, crafting hours, book-club calls, co-working, watch-alongs.

  • Browse & discover real events, filter by in-person/online, category, free.
  • Ticket links out to the official ticket site — we never process payment (keeps us out of refund/consumer-contract liability).
  • "I'm going" — a soft, changeable RSVP, kept private on your phone.
  • Save an event, bring a friend (share), and report anything wrong.
  • Trust signals — a Verified-organiser badge, the ticket platform, and an Unverified flag when a link isn't checked.

FemWell is a directory that surfaces & links events, not the organiser — an important legal line (see §2/§4). FemWell-native meetups (walks, pods) would be the higher-duty tier, built only with the full safety spec enforced.

2 · Safety first, always

The safety model — the rules the feature enforces

Drawn from how Bumble, Meetup, Peanut and Girl Gone International actually keep women safe. This is a numbered spec, and the build honours it:

Venue-level only, never precise.We show the venue or town — never a home address, and we never ask for or store your precise location. (Bumble: don't give out home/office; abstract details.)
Your going-status is private."I'm going" lives on your phone. No other woman, and not us, can see where you'll be — unless you choose to share it.
Public, well-lit venues; daytime first.Every event is a public venue; the interstitial reminds you to meet in daylight the first time, low-pressure (a walk, a tea).
Tell someone where you'll be.One tap shares the event and time with a friend — someone always knows. (Bumble "Share Date" pattern.)
Trust the signals.Verified-organiser + known-platform badges; unverified links flagged. Native events would need a named, present host who passes a liveness check.
Trust your gut, leave any time.Arrange your own way there and back; leave the moment anything feels off — no explanation owed.
Report + block everywhere.Flag an event or organiser; serious conduct routes to us and is signposted to police.
No payment in-app.Every ticket link passes a "you're leaving FemWell — pay only on the official site, never by transfer to a stranger" interstitial.
Legal noteSurface-vs-host + women-only

The Online Safety Act governs the on-platform listings/comments/RSVPs, not the offline meet — but we still owe an illegal-content risk assessment + report/redress. "We surface & link" carries lighter duty than "we host". Women-only rests on the Equality Act single-sex basis (same as the Talk-rooms doc). Both need real UK legal sign-off on the disclaimer + safeguarding copy before public launch.

5 · Online, together

Formats that build belonging from home

For the nervous or lonely, online is the toe-in-the-water — presence without performance. The evidence points to recurring, fixed-slot, low-stakes formats (the parkrun/VIDA lesson):

  • Co-working / body-doubling hours — cameras optional, chat-only welcome, declare-your-intention, no recording. (VIDA runs this for women — direct precedent.)
  • Book-club & watch-along calls — shared low-stakes focus, host-moderated (ties to the Library).
  • Virtual walks / "walk-and-talk" — audio-first, gentle.
  • Live audio rooms by life-domain — Love, Work, Style, the Lighter Side (reuses the Talk-rooms tone).
  • "Cycle-sync" gentle sessions — health-tinted but optional, private RSVP.

Real external online events already appear in the list. FemWell-hosted live circles need live-audio/video we won't rush (a sign-off item) — shown honestly as "coming", never faked.

6 · Warmth & wiring

Why go — and how it ties in

Warm / fun mechanics

  • "Come strangers, leave friends" + a first-timer reassurance — "you won't be the only new one."
  • "Go with someone" — bring a friend now; the opt-in pod ("me too, let's meet at the door") when it's signed off.
  • Fuzzy warmth, never a scoreboard — "a cosy group forming · a few first-timers" not "27 attending."
  • Recurring rituals across life domains — a weekly co-working hour, a monthly city walk, a Friday watch-along.
  • Gentle reminders + post-event reconnection — "lovely having you — find the next one?"
  • Two ways in — every in-person event can have an online sibling to dip a toe first.

How it ties into the app

  • Planner / calendar — "add to my planner" + a gentle reminder (the shared calendar overlay owns the write).
  • Talk rooms — "anywhere good lately?" flows into the Lounge; an event can be shared into a room.
  • Library — book-club calls sit across Events + Library.
  • Jess — "things to do near me", nudges, first-timer reassurance.
  • Life-stage = a tint, never a gate — events span relationships, career, hobbies, joy; health is one flavour, not the filter.
