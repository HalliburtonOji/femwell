Media & readers

Make the cards actually play, actually read, and actually look like us — and never feel empty.

Five gaps, one pass.

1 · Video & 2 · Audio

The cover is the poster. The flora is the visualiser.

Video, inline. The FloraCover is the poster — tap the bloom-play button and the real <video> takes its place and plays in the card (native controls, playsInline, never autoplay, reduced-motion safe). If it's an external platform (TikTok/YouTube), we keep the honest link-out action — no fake embed.

Audio, inline + alive. Real <audio>, plus the bit that makes it ours: a flora visualiser instead of a generic bar meter. A little row of stems topped with blooms — while it plays they sway and pulse, each stem on its own seeded phase so it breathes like a meadow, not a graphic equaliser. Palette: oxblood · gold · sage · cream. Reduced-motion → it simply rests, open and still.

Stems + blooms, swaying on their own phases — a meadow in the wind, not an equaliser. (Time-driven, so it works on any source and never fights CORS.)

3 · Books

Three taps become two.

Tap 1 the book cover card
Tap 2 — "Open reader" → you're already reading: a paged view, right there in the expand, with Next page.
Option "Open the full reader" — the whole experience (chapters, reflections, the club), one clear button.

The expand becomes the reading experience: opening pages fetched via the existing Gutenberg function, paginated to a comfortable page, with next/prev and a page count. It degrades honestly — if the text is slow or unavailable, the pane says so and the full reader is one tap away. Nothing is removed: the full reader keeps everything it has.

4 · The readers

They should look like the app they live in.

Three readers, three different palettes, none of them ours. The craft pass: cream paper (#ECE7DA/#F4EFE3), oxblood headings (#7A1A12), gold/sage flora accents, corner sprigs, Fraunces/Cormorant/Ephesis type — the same world as the cards that open them. Every mechanic stays: chapters, reflections, warnings, progress, the club, flip/movie mode, the cliffhanger countdown.

5 · The summary block

A card should never look empty.

New to the card set (§6.7.7): summary — one real, warm sentence of substance (a hook, a lede, or a true excerpt), not a label.

  • On the cover: under the title, so a card at rest already tells you something worth tapping.
  • In the expand: an "In short" block right under the title, so the page opens with substance instead of air.
  • Fallback ladder: summary → subtitle → excerpt → first line of body — a card is never blank, and never a lorem placeholder.

Joins the block list in §6.7.7 alongside player · excerpt · pull-quote · reading · ingredients · steps. Add a block, never a bespoke card.
