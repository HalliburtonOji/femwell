FemWell · Community · FEATURE #3 · Library / Book Club · brainstorm + build

Read together

Halli's ask: "let users attach books and others talk about it." So the Library becomes a real reading community — keep a shelf, find the women reading the same book, join the season's read — spoiler-safe, no counts, no streaks, no shame. This is the deep brainstorm and what's now built into the live Community's Together shelf, grounded in cited research on how StoryGraph, Fable and the book-club world actually work.

The one-line thesis

Attach a book → find your people around it. A woman shelves what she's reading, sees "others reading this now" (fuzzy, never a number), and drops into a spoiler-safe, moderated per-book thread. The differentiator vs Goodreads is what we refuse to do: no reading goals, no yearly challenge, no "books-read" count, and "Set aside" instead of DNF — the anti-shame that makes women defect from the trackers.

1 · The model

What the Library is

  • Attach a book — search (title/author) or add by hand; it lands on your shelf with a warm on-brand cover.
  • Your shelf, warm states — Reading now · Want to read · Finished (and "Set aside", never "DNF"). No counts, no goals.
  • Talk about it — every book has a moderated readers' corner (a per-book thread) where the women reading it talk — spoiler-safe, kind, anonymous.
  • The season's read — the curated Book Club pick with spoiler-safe checkpoints + "I'm reading this too" and a fuzzy "N reading along".
  • Discover — "books women here love" (whole-life: classics, romance, memoir — never a menopause reading list as the front door).
2 · The evidence (verified sources)

What makes a book community work

ShelfFour warm states · "Set aside" not "DNF"

StoryGraph/Fable/Goodreads all converge on Want / Reading / Finished / DNF — but the thing women defect from Goodreads for is DNF-shame; the top-upvoted asks are literally "DNF not counted toward the goal." "Set aside" reads far warmer. Search-to-attach (one tap, cover appears) is the norm; manual is the fallback.

thestorygraph.com · roadmap.thestorygraph.com (DNF/goal) · bookriot.com (Fable)
APIOpen Library — no key, no creds, no function

The gating fact: Open Library Search + Covers are free, keyless, CORS-friendly and client-callable — so the whole attach surface is buildable with zero external-creds sign-off. Google Books needs a key-in-a-function and its terms forbid charging users; Goodreads' API is dead; ISBNdb is paid. So Open Library is the pick, with metadata cached on attach.

openlibrary.org/developers/api · openlibrary.org/dev/docs/api/covers · developers.google.com/books/terms
SpoilersProgress-locking beats tags

StoryGraph locks a comment to a %/page and "anyone not up to chapter 5 won't see it," rounding up for safety. That's FemWell's existing checkpoint model — generalise it to any book. Add tap-to-reveal spoiler blur as belt-and-braces. Anonymity + async threads + a warm Jess host defuse the "one member dominates" club-killer.

thissplendidshambles.com (buddy reads) · bookriot.com (Fable) · writercosmos.com
CompletionShort picks · async · someone leading

Clubs die from over-long picks (250–350pg completes; 500pg collapses "under guilt about falling behind"), no leader, no variety. Async-first is correct for a small community — StoryGraph buddy reads are fully async. Keep Jess leading, short varied picks, "a few others are reading this now" as the belonging signal.

bookdot.app · viktorcessan.com
DiscoveryMood, not stars; whole-life not clinical

Mood/emotion discovery ("a warm hug", "twisty", "hopeful") is the proven warm model — surface "books women here loved" as fuzzy warmth, never ranked star-counts. Life-stage is a soft optional tint (a "perimenopause memoirs" rail can exist beside "swoony romance" — never as the front door).

whichbook.net · goodreads.com (mood lists) · adventuresinlit.com

5 · Warmth, safety & wiring

Warm, safe, and tied into the app

Warm / fun mechanics (built + planned)

  • Search-to-attach that feels like texting — one tap, a cover appears.
  • "Set aside," never "DNF" — a no-guilt state (v1.1 adds it as a 4th shelf).
  • Fuzzy "others reading this now" — belonging without a number.
  • No counts, goals, streaks or challenges — your profile shows what you read, never how many.
  • Content-warning first — heavy themes flagged before you open (already in the pick entity).
  • Jess hosts every room — no thread is ever empty; no single voice dominates.
  • "Lurking counts" welcome — attach one book you loved as the gentle first step.

Safety (calm on the surface — inherited)

Discussion rides the moderated CommunityPost path (background publish-then-screen, report/block tucked in the "…" menu, botanical aliases); every text input is crisis-checked; spoiler-safe checkpoints by default; content warnings on heavy reads. No new safety friction on the surface.

How it ties in

  • The BookReader — public-domain picks open in the in-app reader; progress feeds the Garden's "reading" days.
  • Together — read-along is a core Together pillar; the season's read shares the "this week, together" rhythm.
  • Profile / Bloomprint — "currently reading" as a gentle identity chip (when the shelf entity lands).
  • Jess — hosts the checkpoints and seeds openers; "what should I read next?" is a natural Jess ask.
  • Life-stage = a soft tint — a "perimenopause memoirs" rail can sit beside romance and thrillers, never the front door.
