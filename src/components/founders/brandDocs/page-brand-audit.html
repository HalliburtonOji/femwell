3 · Planner — the deep dive (Priority #1)

Why it's the worst: PlannerV2Shell.jsx is 6,656 lines carrying the whole planner (week strip, day view, plan-a-day sheet, settings, ritual builder, voice) on the retired palette with a flat background and tiny type. It's the page that most contradicts the brand.

Fix plan (keep the logic, re-skin the surface)

  1. Background: flat fill → PAPER_BG; remove the isolation:isolate stacking-trap (render sheets as root children so none hide behind the nav).
  2. Tokens: guarded sweep of all 113 dusk + 6 gold + 2 cream → canonical (#0B0805 / #A8893F / #ECE7DA), preserving any real phase-hues.
  3. Type: lift all 84 sub-12 to the §1 floor (≥12), on the role scale.
  4. Structure: the content side (intentions, rituals, focus, reminders) becomes the /PlannerCardsDemo card layout — flora hero + summary + Card.jsx rows. The calendar / hour-by-hour day-view stays as the working tool, re-skinned to tokens (not replaced).
  5. Ritual builder: surface it properly (see §5) instead of burying it in a sheet.
  6. v4 add the living-ecosystem flora + a rotating "this week" omen + soulful voice once the brand-identity session lands.

Approach: because it's huge, do it in two demo passes — (a) content side as a card demo, (b) re-skinned calendar — each QA'd at 390px before merge. Largest single effort in this plan.

4 · Jess — visual rebuild (Priority #2)

Keep the brain, rebuild the body. The agent wiring works (the personal_assistant agent + inline answers). The shell is what's off — legacy palette, no paper, generic chat chrome.

Rebuild plan

  1. Hero: a soulful sigil-halo bloom (blush→gold→sage) + the carved heart + Ephesis "Jess" + a smart-friend one-liner + the privacy / not-medical line.
  2. Surface: PAPER_BG; retire the legacy palette → canonical tokens; lift all 30 sub-12 fonts.
  3. Conversation as cards: answers and starter prompts rendered in the Card.jsx language — life-spanning prompts (dating / career / friendship / fun, not just symptoms), each deep-linking the exact surface.

Coordination: confirm no other session is mid-flight on Jess's wiring before the re-skin; I touch visual/markup only.

5 · Ritual Builder — surface it + make it cross-page

Today it's hidden inside the Planner shell (a sheet behind a button). The rituals themselves are lovely and lightweight — a moment of resta glass of watera gentle stretcha mindful pause — and the code already supports context-aware selection (RitualsForContext). That's the seed of a real cross-page feature.

Recommendation

  1. Surface on Today: a LogActionCard "a small ritual for right now" in the day flow — one tap to do/log a micro-ritual, phase- and time-aware. The hearth page is where a daily ritual belongs.
  2. Make it cross-page (the real win): the same ritual card, contextualised per surface — Journal → "three quiet lines"; Nutrition → "a glass of water"; Pulse → "a mindful pause"; Community → "send one kind message"; Garden → "tend your companion". One shared component (RitualCard) fed by RitualsForContext(page, phase, timeOfDay).
  3. Tie to the garden & streaks: completing rituals waters the companion / earns a pollinator visit (the §6 ecosystem) — so rituals become the connective habit loop across the app, not a buried Planner tool.
  4. Builder stays in Planner (compose your own ritual set) but is promoted to a clear entry, and its output feeds the cross-page cards.

Effort: a shared RitualCard + the context resolver is M; surfacing on Today is S. I'd prototype it as a demo on Today first.
