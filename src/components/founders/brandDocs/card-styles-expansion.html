The brief

You want more card variety than our current set. This is a plan (not a build): the research, then a proposed expanded card-style library, each adapted to our brand — cream paper, deep-oxblood headings, gold, flora, warm plain voice. On approval it folds into the bible's card-language (§6.7.0).

Your references, in one line each: a Monzo-style bento home (different-sized tiles) · dismissible suggestion cards (an ✕) · a rich "Loans · On track" card (progress ring + illustration + inline actions + a "…" menu) · section headers · and a Netflix-style media carousel (big hero cards with imagery + a "No.1 in Films" badge + a featured area, then a smaller category row).

One · The set we have

Six, already good.

Our current card language (bible §6.7.0 / §6.10): the standard FwCard · the clipboard board (big framed board + horizontal board-slider) · stacked top/bottom demarcations (each its own horizontal sub-slider) · tile-grid in a board · accent-rim sub-card · focused colour pills · the in-card horizontal deck. This plan adds to that — a bounded vocabulary, each new type with a distinct job.

Two · The expanded library

Nine new card styles.

1 · Bento mixed-size tiles

A grid of different-sized tiles — one big hero tile + several small ones — so a page overview is glanceable, not a uniform grid. (Your Monzo reference.)

Today
Day 14
follicular · rising
Water
4 / 6 glasses
Mood
tap to log
Chapter
2 min read
Garden
a bee visited

Anatomy: a 2-col grid, a big 2-row hero tile (the day/most-important) + 1×1 tiles; each tile = icon/flora + a label + one number/status, taps to the full thing. When: a page's at-a-glance overview (Today, Profile, Garden). Rule: vary the size for hierarchy — never all-uniform; fill the grid, no empty cells.

2 · Media / hero card + category row

A large featured card with imagery/flora + a small badge ("New" / "No.1 this week"), then a smaller category row of thumbnails beneath. (Your Netflix reference.)

No.1 this week
Daily story
The Garden at Dusk
A five-minute read for a soft evening.
Read nowSave
More to readSee all ›
On restMoney & youA love letterFriendship

Anatomy: a hero card (cover area with a flora/soft-gradient image + a small ribbon badge) → title + line + a primary action; then a labelled shelf (title + "See all" + a peeking row of thumbnails with dots + ‹ › arrows). When: Lifestyle (Read/Listen/Watch), Programs, Daily Story. Brand: imagery is flora/soft paper gradients, not stock photos; badges are quiet gold/sage, not loud red.

3 · Category row / shelf

The labelled horizontally-scrolling shelf on its own (title + See-all + a row of cards + peek + arrows) — the workhorse for "a row of things in a category".

Tonight's calmSee all ›
Wind-downSleep storyBreatheBath ritual
‹›

When: any "row of peers in a category" — recipes, programmes, community rooms, deals. Rule: 12–16% peek + dots + tappable ‹ › arrows (our §6.10.3 standard); cap ~8, best first; a "See all" opens the full grid.

4 · Rich stat / status card

One rich card = a progress ring (or mini-chart) + a flora illustration + inline action buttons + a status badge + a quiet "…" menu. (Your "Loans · On track".)

⋯
This cycle · goals
On track 3 of 4
Your money-plant's nearly in bud — one more week.
Tend itSee steps

Anatomy: a ring/chart (in a phase hue or gold) + a flora motif + a serif title + a status badge ("On track"/"3 of 4") + two inline actions + a top-right "…" overflow (edit/snooze/hide). When: Pulse, Programs progress, Goals, Health summaries. Brand: the ring reads as growth (paired with the flora lifecycle), never a clinical dial; the "…" is quiet, not a loud kebab.

5 · Dismissible suggestion card

A gentle nudge you can dismiss with an ✕ (or snooze) — so suggestions never nag.

✕
A small idea
You've journalled 4 evenings running.
Want a gentle 9pm nudge to keep it? No pressure.
Yes, remind meNot now

Anatomy: a warm suggestion + an ✕ (dismiss) / "Not now" (snooze) + a light accent rim (sage). When: Today nudges, Community prompts, Nutrition tips. Rules: dismiss sticks (don't re-show the same one); the ✕ has a real ≥44px target + label for a screen reader; never guilt.

6 · Expandable card (tap to open)

Summary first, tap to expand in place for the detail — no route change.

Your Health · Letter II
Your heart, this season
⌄
A short letter — the key facts, then tap for the full read + your GP prep.

When: Health letters, Journal entries, FAQ-style content, a summary that some want expanded. Rule: expands in place (animated height, reduced-motion safe); a clear ⌄/⌃ affordance.

Three · Keep it FemWell

Adapt, don't copy.

The references are dark, dense, photo-heavy fintech/streaming. We take the arrangement, not the look:

Reference doesFemWell does instead
Dark charcoal cards, neon accentsCream paper (PAPER_BG), gold hairlines, one accent rim per card
Stock photography / poster artFlora illustration + soft paper gradients as the "imagery"
Loud red/green status, big kebabsQuiet gold/sage badges, a small "…" menu, oxblood headings
Dense data dialsProgress rings paired with the flora lifecycle (growth, not a clinical gauge)
Punchy marketing voiceWarm plain smart-friend voice on every label

A new card earns its place with a new JOB, not a new colour — the vocabulary stays bounded (~12–15 named types), each with a seeded demo.

Six · Rules kept + next step

Same discipline, more range.

Unchanged: the MAIN page slider stays HORIZONTAL · no long empty space (bento fills / stacked demarcations) · in-card decks keep the safeguards (12–16% peek + dots + ‹ › arrows + `overscroll-behavior-x:contain`) · one accent rim per card · typed cards never empty · warm voice, no emoji, oxblood headings.
