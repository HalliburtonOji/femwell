The interaction, spelled out

  1. Default: item #1 (best/newest) is the hero; the rest sit in the rail below, peeking off the right edge.
  2. Tap a rail thumbnail → the hero cross-fades (≈220ms) and the tapped item becomes the hero; the previous hero animates down into the rail's front slot.
  3. The rail stays a horizontal slider (swipe / ‹ › arrows / dots), with the §6.10.3 peek + See-all discipline — "See all" opens the full grid (the reliable second path, since horizontal scroll gets missed).
  4. One tag ON the hero art ("No.1 this week" / "New"), quiet chrome so the flora cover reads.

Where it shines: Lifestyle (Read / Listen / Watch / Daily Story), Programs (featured programme + shelves), Community (featured room). The single most reusable "browse a category" surface.

The lead pattern has a name — and rules

The hero+rail is the image-gallery / "thumbnail-to-hero" pattern (a thumbnail overview + a primary viewer). Best-practice, folded into the lead mock: highlight the current item so she keeps her place (we do — it leaves the rail and becomes the hero); show all thumbs at ≤10–14, and if you truncate you MUST peek/"+N" (else people think the visible set is all there is); crossfade ≤220ms (we use 220 — a promotion, not a scene change); never autoplay; keyboard-completable focus.
