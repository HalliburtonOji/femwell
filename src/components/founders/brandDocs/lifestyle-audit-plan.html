Lifestyle — deep audit + redesign & substance plan

Plan-first (no live Lifestyle code changed). Same treatment as Community + Nutrition: onto the §6.8.2 canonical template, and deepen every feature. Rev 2 folds in Halli’s craft direction (2026-07-14): well-formatted clipboard that holds big rich cards; on-brand illustrated flora covers for articles (retire stock photos), curated — quality over quantity; format everything well; and a build process of small pieces, built slowly to a high finish, one at a time.

TL;DR

The live page (LifestyleEliteShell) is good but a generation behind — on the older §6.8 v1 signature (flipped 2026-06-28), missing the controller cards, the glance⇆Jess rotating digest, and the “Handy right now” quick row. Deeper: the personalisation engine is unwired (“Picked for you” = top engagement only); the immersive Daily Story reader and the ~15-section Horoscope reader (+ birth-chart onboarding) were dropped and are dark in prod; and there’s no real video content.

Halli’s craft direction, now the spine of this plan: (1) the clipboard boards must be formatted to hold BIG rich cards — e.g. an Articles board of large article cards, not cramped one-line tiles (which is what’s there now); (2) article covers must be on-brand illustrated flora/garden + colour-graded write-up covers, never stock photos, and there should be fewer, curated articles; (3) every surface formatted with care; (4) we build in small, well-defined pieces — each finished properly before the next, not big sweeping rewrites.

Almost all of it is feasible with code that already exists: the big-card primitive (FwCard 365×488 / ArticleCard), the flora + colourway system for covers, and the personalisation + reader functions. Plan adds no new backend.

The big-card language the brand mandates (§6.7: FwCard 365×488 with cover + hook + line + inline action + corner sprigs; ArticleCard/StoryCard wrap it) is never used inside the boards. So the Read board reads like a list, not a considered shelf of articles. Fix in §3.

craft #2 Article covers are stock photos. Across the domain, covers prefer an external image_url (stock/OG image), only falling back to image_gradient / getCategoryGradient(category) (EditorialHero.jsx:20, LifestyleDetail.jsx:348-354). That’s off-world — the rest of the app is an illustrated flora garden. Halli: covers must be on-brand flora + colour-graded write-up covers, no stock. Fix in §3.

headline gap The personalisation engine is unplugged. The shell reads a flat -engagement_score sort and has zero references to getLifestyleFeed / LifestyleProfile / LifestyleInteractions / recordLifestyleAction — all of which exist and work. “Picked for you” and “For your phase” aren’t tuned to her; her taps teach nothing.

dark in prod Two of the richest readers were dropped.

3 · Craft & formatting spec (Halli’s direction)

3.1 — The clipboard, formatted to hold BIG rich cards

Keep the ClipboardSlider board frame (it’s on-brand and on-spec), but change what lives inside a board. Today a board is a StackedCard of two sub-sliders of tiny ContentRows. Instead, a content board’s body becomes a horizontal deck of BIG cards (FwCard/ArticleCard, 365×488) — the CardDeck / HorizontalPeekRail pattern (§6.10.1): one big card per page, edge-peek of the next, its own dots + ‘ › arrows.

Two board archetypes (a board is one or the other, never a cramped mix):

  • Showcase board (Read, Listen, Story) — a deck of big cards: cover + eyebrow + title + one real line/snippet + an inline action (play / save / open). This is where formatting matters most.
  • Utility board (The good life, For you’s Try-this/phase) — the existing StackedCard of interactive lenses stays; it’s a doing surface, not a shelf.

Formatting rules (enforce all): uniform card size on a row (§6.7.0) — no card a different height/width; real card chrome only (gradient paper wash + accent rim + 4-corner sprigs); a cover on every showcase card; a hook + a real line (never a bare title); an inline action; generous internal padding; the deck breathes (peek + arrows), it doesn’t cram. Curated counts — ~4–6 big cards per showcase deck, not 20; the shelf should feel chosen.

3.2 — Article covers: on-brand flora, never stock

Build one small, reusable FloraCover component and make it the default cover everywhere (article cards, the Read deck, the LifestyleDetail hero) — retire external image_url for FemWell write-ups. It composes, entirely in-app (no fetch, no stock), from primitives that already exist:

  • Colour grade — a paper-to-colourway gradient (linear-gradient(160deg, paperHi → {colourway}~14%)), the colourway chosen from the item’s category / emotional_tag (map the 27 categories → the §2.5 colourways). This is the “colour-graded write-up cover”.
  • Flora motif — a RichBloomV2 headOnly spray or a CardFrame corner sprig / small bough in that colourway, chosen deterministically per item (seed from id so a piece always wears the same face) — the same garden language as the hero + Daily Story gradients.
  • Title lockup — the piece title in Fraunces (or Ephesis for stories) over the grade, with the eyebrow (type · read-time) — so the cover itself carries the write-up, not a photo.
  • Restraint — one bloom, one grade, a hairline; never busy. Reduced-motion safe. Matches §4 (botanical brand-image) + §5 (bloom craft).

Priority flip: today it’s image_url ? stock : gradient. Change to FloraCover first for FemWell-authored items; a real photo is allowed only for genuine external editorial where it’s the point (and even then, colour-graded into the world). No raw stock on the shelf.

Curation (quality over quantity): Halli — “not too many articles.” The Read deck shows a small, chosen set (~5), personalised (§4), each with a real FloraCover and a genuine hook. Fewer, better, beautifully covered beats an endless list. Tune generateFemwellContent volume down + lean on curation, not firehose.

6 · Build process & order — small pieces, high finish, one at a time

How we build this (Halli’s direction): not a big sweeping rebuild. We take one small, well-defined piece at a time, brainstorm it focused and narrow, build it substantially and properly, get it to a high finish (Ms Atelier craft + Ms Verify), then move to the next. Each piece below is self-contained and shippable on its own; the page improves visibly with every one. No piece starts until the previous is finished.

Pieces, in order (each is small on purpose):

  1. The FloraCover component. One reusable on-brand cover (colour grade + flora motif + title lockup, deterministic per id). Built + previewed in isolation first.Finish bar: renders for any item/category, no stock, reduced-motion safe, matches §4–5; shown across 6 categories.
  2. The big ArticleCard + the showcase deck. Wrap FwCard with FloraCover; lay a CardDeck/peek-rail of ~5 into one board body. Read board only.Finish bar: uniform big cards, cover + hook + line + inline Save + Open-full, peek + arrows, no cramping.
  3. Real personalisation behind the Read deck. Feed it from getLifestyleFeed; log Save/Open via recordLifestyleAction.Finish bar: deck reflects followed topics/phase; a save visibly shifts next load.
  4. The Handy-right-now row (V2QuickRow). Smallest band; port + wire ~6 real jumps.Finish bar: every chip a real action; on-brand compact chrome.
  5. The controller-card hero (band 1). Port the renderer; 5 destinations re-bloom + re-title + action.Finish bar: tap re-blooms one→many, re-tints, phase pill + action correct; one bloom, no crowding bouquet.
  6. The glance⇆Jess row (band 2) + lifestyleJessSummary. Two equal panels; digest rotates; inner sheet slides up.Finish bar: digest changes each load; sheet never a wall; panels equal height.
  7. Assemble LifestyleV2Shell + flip the route. Compose bands 0–5, keep all boards, one-line pages.config flip after Verify (revert kept).Finish bar: full page on §6.8.2, nothing stripped, live-walked at 390px.
  8. Restore the Daily Story reader + fix the Today reel deep-links.Finish bar: the immersive reader opens from Story; the reel lands on the right chapter.
  9. Restore the Horoscope reader + birth-chart onboarding.Finish bar: full reader opens from Sky; a new user can set up a chart.
  10. Convert the remaining boards to big-card / rich formatting (Listen, Story&sky, For-you) with flora covers.Finish bar: no board still on cramped tiles; every showcase card covered.
  11. De-hardcode + go whole-life. Rotate Try-this / A-day / permission / external pods; add life-domain chips.Finish bar: copy varies per load; life domains visibly present.
  12. Fill the real media lane (content-ops via existing ingestion).Finish bar: real videos/podcasts play in-card, curated.

Pieces 1–11 need no new backend or entity. Piece 1 (FloraCover) and piece 2 (big article cards) come first on purpose — they’re the craft Halli called out and are visible immediately.
