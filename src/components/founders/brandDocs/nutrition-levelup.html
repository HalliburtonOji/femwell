2 · The frontier (cited, 2024–26)

The deeper evidence

Beyond the level-1 basics (iron flagship, cycle-aware-not-rules, commensality, menopause protein — all already cited). Each row flagged evidence / popular-practice / myth.

01Photo logging: ID is solved, portion isn't

Vision models hit 92–98% on food identification but real-world portion estimation falls as low as 39% — and portion drives calories. Best systems land ~10–20% volume error; micronutrients are unreliable (vit C r≈0.30). → Photo logs should show a calorie/macro RANGE, never iron/micros, and always "estimate → you confirm". evidence

Systematic review (PMC 2024) · JMIR 2024 — portion estimation

02The accuracy ceiling is the data, not the UI

UK CoFID is ~3,300 generic foods, updated rarely, and omits omega-3/6, folates, vitamin D. Open Food Facts is "as sold" (dry pasta, not cooked) and packaged-only. The serious-app fix is composite/recipe-level nutrition — build a dish from ingredients with cooking-yield factors. → Route real cooked meals through a "build from ingredients" path; keep OFF for barcodes only. evidence

Nutrition Bulletin — CoFID limits · OFF "as sold" gap

03Condition personalisation that's actually evidenced

PCOS: inositol strongest in insulin-resistant phenotype (~metformin, fewer GI effects). Endometriosis: no diet has RCT superiority — Mediterranean / low-FODMAP may ease pain; influencer elimination diets are under-supported. Hashimoto's: Mediterranean > blanket gluten-free. Perimenopause: women under-eat fibre + vit D + calcium + iron, and "the window" is peri, not post. → Per-condition nutrient watch-lists, evidence-graded, with explicit anti-myth caution. evidence

PCOS guideline 2023 · Endo diet review 2025 · Perimenopause intakes

04Pregnancy/GDM & lactation — a safety + trust win

GDM: NICE 2025 issued a research recommendation because there's no proven GDM diet; low-carb showed no benefit. → Route GDM users to their NHS dietitian + low-GI; never ship a low-carb GDM plan as "proven". Lactation: exclusive breastfeeding needs ~+500 kcal/day; below ~2,700 kcal calcium/zinc/B6/folate run low. → A gentle lactation energy uplift + a "don't drop below" guardrail. evidence

NICE NG3 (GDM) · Lactation energy needs

05The GLP-1 era: protect muscle, don't sell a diet

~25–40% of GLP-1 weight loss is lean mass (women + older adults higher-risk); protein + resistance training preserve it. → An opt-in "GLP-1 muscle-guardian" mode: protein-forward small meals, micros on a shrunken appetite, hydration, "pair with strength" — framed as protection, never weight-loss. evidence

ACE 2025 — GLP-1 & lean mass · ENDO 2025

06Intuitive eating beats the diet-apps — and is evidenced

2024 reviews link intuitive/mindful eating to less disordered eating + depression, better body image, lower BMI, more fruit/veg/fibre (pooled effect ~1.50). Meanwhile Noom (anti-diet branding, calorie-counting reality; $62M billing settlement) and Yuka (A–E food grading → documented orthorexia risk) are the anti-patterns. ZOE's microbiome explains only ~6–7% of glucose/lipid variation at ~£280/kg lost. → Make intuitive-eating the default; never grade foods A–E; ship an ED-safe "numbers off" mode. evidence + competitor hype

Intuitive eating review 2024 · Yuka critique · ZOE trial scrutiny

The market gap, in one line

ZOE · Noom · MyFitnessPal · Lifesum · Cronometer · Yuka are not cycle/life-stage-aware in a clinically-grounded, anti-shame way — and women report failing them at high rates. That unserved space — female-physiology-aware nutrition that refuses diet-culture mechanics — is exactly what +2 builds.

5 · The one gated call

Photo → macros needs your OK

Everything else in +1/+2 rides existing entities and the curated/composite approach — no new function. The single exception is photo→macros: it's a multimodal vision-model call, the one thing the "no new base44 function" rule reserves for your sign-off.

  • Why a function is unavoidable: each photo is a metered model invocation (real per-call cost), and accuracy degrades on mixed/cultural dishes — unlimited free snapping is both expensive and quality-risky.
  • The safe shape if you approve: a capped/metered vision function (e.g. N free photo-logs/day), cached per image, with a graceful "switch to quick-search" fallback for low-confidence dishes; outputs a range + "estimate, you're the editor" disclosure; never drives iron/micros.
  • If you'd rather not: the honest text-nudge stays (it's fine), and we ship every other +1/+2 item with zero rule-risk.

Flag: this is the only item that needs a new gated capability. Recommendation: approve it metered — photo logging is the #1 requested logger feature and the honest "range + confirm" framing turns its weakness into a brand-trust moment. Your call.
