Calm on the surface

Safety was too in-your-face — a helpline under every post, disclaimers everywhere, report buttons shouting on every card. Mature apps don't do that, and it made FemWell feel heavier than it is. New principle: safe by design, calm on the surface — keep every bit of protection, move it into the background, and give the surface back to warmth.

The principle (Community-wide)

The protection stays; the friction goes. Strong moderation runs silently in the backend. Safety controls are available but tucked away. Crisis help appears only in context, or in one quiet corner — never as a banner on every post. Every Community feature inherits this.

The four rules
Rule 1Moderation is invisible

Publish-then-screen + crisis detection run in the backend on every post, silently. The user feels a kind room, not a screening process. (Mumsnet keeps intervention minimal; Elpha hid-first, reviewed-after — the mature pattern.)

Rule 2Report & block are tucked away

No Flag icon shouting on every post. Report + "hide this voice" live behind a quiet "…" menu — there the instant you need them, invisible until then.

Rule 3Crisis help is contextual

The crisis check still fires — but only when the words actually warrant it, as a gentle sheet. No helpline printed under every post. One calm "Feeling low? Find support" link lives in the footer, once.

Rule 4No disclaimer walls

"Peer support, not medical advice" and the like appear once, in a quiet Support corner — not stamped under every reply box. Meet-safe reminders appear in context (when you're about to get a ticket for an in-person event), not as a permanent banner.
