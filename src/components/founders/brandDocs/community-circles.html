Believed here

Circles are the smaller rooms — for who you are (life stages), what you're living with (PCOS, endo, PMDD), and what you love (books, career, creativity, movement). This is the deep brainstorm for making them robust, warm and safe. The best of them give a woman one thing a big room can't: "here it's believed." Cited research inside; nothing built yet — this is the plan for your reaction.

The one-line thesis

The killer for a small community isn't too few features — it's an empty circle. So Circles v1 is about aliveness and warmth: every live circle is seeded so it's never at zero, has a Jess host who guarantees a first reply, a warm first-post welcome ("you're not too much"), a gentle whole-life discovery card (a Creativity circle beside a Menopause one), and — for the sensitive ones — a calm NHS-grounded info anchor + an author-set content-warning veil. Almost all of it runs on entities we already have.

1 · Starting point (already built)

What Circles are today

A curated catalogue of 12 circles in three groups — Life stages (TTC · pregnancy · postpartum · perimenopause · menopause), Living with (PCOS · endo · PMDD, flagged sensitive with consent-on-join), Shared loves (books · career · creativity · movement). Each is lurkable (read without joining), join-to-post, with a device-local join and a `CircleMembership` entity; posts are circle-scoped `CommunityPost`; discovery already suggests circles from life-stage + interests. The bones are right — this feature makes them alive and warm, not just present.

2 · The evidence (verified sources)

What makes a cohort work

AlivenessEmpty = death; variety of voices is the survival signal

A community needs only 10–20 active people to feel alive, but only ~10–30% show to any thread — so too many circles dilutes below the floor. The earliest survival predictor is poster variety, visible within 2 hours of a channel's life; the killer is a no-recent-posts feed that reads "dead." Seed every live circle.

medium.com/together-institute · dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1718918.1718932 (ACM CSCW)
"Believed here"The specific benefit of a condition cohort

PCOS study: women valued talking "with people who completely understand," got information their clinicians lacked, and felt "want to fight it even more." That's the exact promise of the endo/PCOS/PMDD circles — validation you can't get elsewhere.

pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/PMC3867626
CuratedCurated beats user-created for a small, safe app

Mumsnet is staff-curated topics; HealthUnlocked condition communities are charity-hosted + moderated. Curation removes creation-moderation, dead-circle sprawl and vanity surfaces. Keep it curated — don't add a "create a circle" button.

mumsnet.com/talk · healthunlocked.com/communities
First postWelcome it, per circle

First-post care is the biggest retention lever — a "congrats on your first post" + "let me know if you have questions" is "the difference between a member who disappears and one who becomes a regular." In an anonymous app the buddy = Jess + a warm auto-welcome.

fluentcommunity.co/blog/community-onboarding-strategies
DiscoveryA few for you, whole-life, condition circles last

"Surface only the most relevant, don't bombard." A short opt-in taste survey is the least-creepy matcher. Crucial guardrail: never infer a condition circle from tracked symptoms (creepy + special-category), and interleave a Shared-loves circle beside any stage/condition one so discovery never reads "here's your illness."

bettermode.com · fluentcommunity.co
MisinfoThe no-upvote quality lever

Misinformation in condition groups is "a matter of public-health concern"; it's countered by peer correction + surfacing good info. FemWell has no upvotes (k-anon), so it needs a different lever: a calm NHS/charity info anchor per condition circle + easy peer correction + report.

jmir.org/2025/1/e71140 (scoping review)
3 · The plan

Robust v1 — seven pillars

#Pillar · why
1Seed every live circle — Jess "first light" post + a circle-specific starter so none is ever empty. (The single highest-impact anti-death move.)
2Per-circle Jess host + "no post left unanswered" — reuses the backstop just shipped for Talk rooms. A first reply is guaranteed.
3Warm first-post welcome — "congrats on your first word" + "you're not too much / it's believed here" (sensitive circles).
4Per-condition NHS/charity info anchor — one calm grounded line atop each condition circle (the misinformation counterweight).
5"A few circles for you" discovery card — whole-life interleaved, condition circles offered gently and last, never inferred from symptoms.
6Author-set content-warning veil on heavy sensitive posts — neutral, "tap to read," never a hard block.
7Circle ritual of the week — one low-barrier rotating prompt per circle (Books: "what wrecked you"; TTC: "one kind thing in the wait"; Peri: "the symptom no one warned you about").

Plus the shared calm-safety floor already in Community (report/hide in the "…" menu, background moderation, botanical aliases, reaction-only/thread-lock, k-anon, no counts) — and a brand pass so the Circles surfaces match §6.8.2 like the rest.

4 · The careful bit

Sensitive circles — safe AND warm

  • Consent-on-join (built) + add "leaving removes you from the member list" (UK GDPR posture — these are special-category data).
  • Never infer a condition circle from tracked symptoms — self-declaration or self-open only.
  • NHS/charity info anchor per condition — the quality lever in a no-upvote app.
  • Content-warning veil — neutral, preparatory, optional (reduces anxiety-contagion for readers).
  • Tint the condition circle with life — recipes, dating, work belong in a PCOS circle too, so it never curdles into a symptom feed.
  • Warm first-timer tone — "believed here," never clinical.
  • Legal review of the sensitive-consent + single-sex/age wording before public launch (flagged, not legal advice).
5 · Build reality

What's buildable now vs needs sign-off

Almost everything is buildable now on the entities we already have — the curated `CIRCLES` catalogue, `CircleMembership`, circle-scoped `CommunityPost`, Jess, and `suggestedCircles`. No new DB entity, no external creds. The seeding, host, welcome, info anchor, discovery card and ritual prompts all reuse existing paths.

buildable now: seed circles · Jess host + backstop · first-post welcome · info anchor · discovery card · ritual prompts · brand pass.

one small schema field (sign-off): the author-set content-warning veil (pillar 6) needs a new `CommunityPost.content_warning` field (a lean add, no external creds). Everything else needs nothing.

Do NOT add a `Circle` entity — the curated-catalogue-in-code decision stays. This pass would be one build on top of the Community safety floor. Nothing here is built yet — awaiting your go on the plan (and the one content-warning field).
