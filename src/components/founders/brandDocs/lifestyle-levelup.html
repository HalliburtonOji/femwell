2 · The evidence (cited, 2024–26)

Leisure is medicine — especially for her

Each row flagged evidence / popular-practice.

01The women's leisure gap is the whole "why"

UK men get ~5 more leisure hours/week, and women's leisure is more fragmented and guilt-laden. → Make sanctioned permission/rest the emotional spine — not "more content". evidence

ONS — the leisure gap

025-minute "micro-acts of joy" actually work

UCSF's Big Joy Project (JMIR, June 2025): seven days of 5–10-minute daily micro-acts (gratitude, a kind act, awe) raised wellbeing + positive emotion, lowered stress, improved sleep — the first short intervention to show such strong benefits. → Size the dopamine menu's smallest tier around the 5-minute micro-act — the sweet spot for women with no spare hour. evidence

UCSF — Big Joy Project

03The awe walk is a packaged, RCT-backed prescription

A 15-minute "look for wonder" walk increased awe, joy, compassion and gratitude (Sturm/Keltner, Emotion 2020); 2024 follow-ups show awe improves quality of life and mood. → Ship an "Awe walk" card (15 min, one prompt: "find one vast thing") — a literal evidence-based intervention. evidence

Harvard — awe walks · Awe RCT 2025

04Making things lowers cortisol — skill irrelevant

45 minutes of art-making reduced cortisol regardless of artistic skill (Drexel); mind-stimulating leisure links to longevity; the creative-health field counts 600+ mechanisms (Fancourt). → A MAKE lane (craft, doodle, slow-living) is wellbeing, not decoration. evidence

awe/affect 2025 · Fancourt creative-health review (companion research file)

05Reading for pleasure is NHS-credible wellbeing

The Reading Agency's Reading Well scheme prescribes books like prescriptions: 92% found their book helpful, 90% of health professionals endorsed it, 3.8M+ borrowed. A 2024 study frames reading-for-pleasure as salutogenic. → Frame Daily Story + Read as reading-for-wellbeing, themed shelves, warm — with a light "not a substitute for care" footing where a story touches a hard theme. evidence

Reading Well · Reading & wellbeing 2024 · caution: don't medicalise

06Abundance without paywall-anxiety — and NO streaks

Insight Timer's model is a huge free library (80+ new free meditations daily) — abundance, not a paywall. But Calm/Headspace/Insight all use streaks that re-import guilt. → Copy the free-abundance feel (our PD/CC firehoses replicate it for free); explicitly BAN punishing streaks on leisure — celebrate return, never shame absence. adapt / avoid

3 · The priority

The content-supply fix

Your cards link out today because nothing verifies embeddability before storing a row, and the shelves aren't fed from free libraries. Three moves fix it — no CMS team.

Move 1 · An embeddability gate at ingest

Before a row is ever stored, verify it actually plays: YouTube Data API status.embeddable === true + UK not in regionRestriction.blocked; a free zero-auth oEmbed 200-with-html pre-check; for audio, a real RSS <enclosure> MP3 or an Acast/Buzzsprout embed. Store embed_verified_at + region_ok as hard columns. the single biggest lever

YouTube Data API — status.embeddable · oEmbed

Move 2 · Three free firehoses (legal, commercial-OK)

SourceLaneWhy
LibriVox (API)Listen / RESTPublic-domain audiobooks & poetry — "use however you wish, including commercial, no credit needed". Instant, infinite sleep/calm supply for your real HTML5 player.
Standard Ebooks (OPDS)ReadBeautifully typeset public-domain books — a far more credible Read card than raw gutendex HTML.
ccMixter / Free Music ArchiveAwe / ambienceCC-BY ambient beds (store the one-line attribution) — turns a static awe image into a 60-second "stay here a minute".

LibriVox PD + API · Standard Ebooks · dig.ccMixter (commercial-OK)

Move 3 · Real podcast supply (Spotify stays link-out)

Spotify embeds give logged-out listeners only a 30-second preview — so it's a "preview + open full episode" link-out, never your in-card listen. The hosts that give a full-episode, no-login iframe are Acast (no cookies, no login, counts as a real download) and Buzzsprout. → Source UK women's-wellness shows hosted on Acast/Buzzsprout, or pull the public RSS <enclosure> MP3 into your existing seek player.

Acast embed · Buzzsprout iframe · Spotify 30-sec preview

7 · Honest build reality

What's rule-safe vs the one gated call

  • Rule-safe (no new function): seeding the libraries (curated, offline-verified like today's 6 videos), the new lanes, the intentions read-back shelf, real Guides, the dopamine menu depth, TTS "listen to this", the library-card onboarding. All ride existing entities (LifestyleItems, PlannerItems, SkyNote, saved_item_ids).
  • The one gated call: an automated verify-and-sweep job (weekly oEmbed/HEAD re-check that auto-demotes dead rows + pulls a replacement) is the only piece that wants a small scheduled function (~150 lines). v1 doesn't need it — we can hand-verify the curated library exactly like the live 6 videos. Flag it for your OK only when the library grows past hand-maintenance.
  • Licensing posture: LibriVox (PD, no credit), Standard Ebooks (PD), gutendex (PD), ccMixter/FMA (CC-BY, store attribution) — all commercial-OK. Spotify stays link-out (30-sec preview reality).
