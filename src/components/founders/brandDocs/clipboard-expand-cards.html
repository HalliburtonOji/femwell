Browse, then dive

A "tap a card → it opens into a whole rich page" interaction for our sliding clipboard — Breeze's expand pattern, made unmistakably FemWell and kept to content.

The idea

A carousel you browse, and cards that open all the way up.

Breeze (the dating app) does one thing beautifully: a horizontal carousel of cards with pager dots, and when you tap a card it expands into a full-screen detail card — a big cover, a title, an inline "anthem" player, detail rows, interest chips, and sticky primary actions. The motion and information shape are lovely. We take those, and drop the dating entirely.

In FemWell it becomes: a ClipboardSlider of flora-cover content cards → tap one → it opens into a beautiful page with a FloraCover hero, a Fraunces title, an inline player (a story/session that plays in the card), warm detail rows, topic chips, and sticky actions (Save · Play · Open) — with a smooth expand and a back to collapse. Same premium feel; no people, no photos, no swiping.

The hard line — read this

This pattern is for CONTENT. Not people.

The reference is a dating app, so the temptation is "cards of women you tap to open." We do not do that here. People & connection in FemWell live behind the safety rails — anonymity by default, k-anon presence, no cold-DMs, consent-gated messaging, report/block, screened-for-kindness. A tap-to-open "profile card" would bulldoze all of that.

So this demo stays on content: reads, listens, guided sessions, books, recipes, and content rooms (a topic circle you peek into, not a person). If we ever want this shape for connection, it's a separate build that routes every open + action through the DM/connection rails — flagged, not assumed.

Seven honest homes for it (all content).

It elevates the clipboard from "cards that link out" to "cards that contain a whole experience" — the open feels like arriving, not navigating away. Great for a Discover/for-you rail that spans life domains.

The expand animation

Three ways to open — and what I built.

OptionFeelCost
A · Scale-fade overlay builtthe page fades + scales up (0.96→1) from centre, cover animates in. Premium, calm, GPU-cheap.Low. Reduced-motion safe (snaps).
B · Shared-element FLIPthe tapped card morphs — its cover grows into the full-screen hero (most "magic", closest to Breeze).Higher — measure card rect, animate to full-screen, reconcile. Worth it if this becomes core.
C · Slide-up sheetthe detail rises from the bottom like a sheet.Low, but reads as "a sheet", not "the card became a page".

I shipped A for the demo — it delivers ~90% of the feeling at a fraction of the complexity, and it's reduced-motion-safe. B (FLIP) is the upgrade if Halli wants the true card-morph; noted as the next step, not built.

The rest of the polish

  • Edge-peek + pager dots — the carousel shows a sliver of the next card and a dot rail (reusing ClipboardSlider), so it reads as "there's more".
  • Inline player — a real play/pause with a progress bar that fills over the run-time (simulated in the demo; wires to real audio later). This is the reference's "anthem", reframed as a story/session.
  • Sticky actions — Save + one primary (Play/Read/Begin/Cook/Open) pinned to the bottom over a cream fade, always reachable.
  • Brand, not dating — FloraCover scenes (no stock photos), Fraunces/Cormorant/Ephesis type, oxblood titles, meaning-blooms + a drifting pollinator, cream paper world.
