One flower, blooming through the day

The tap-to-promote hero, merged INTO the floral hero itself. Built · live on /NutritionV2Demo

The separate big “promote” card is gone. The small cards move up to sit where the little bloom used to be, and they become the controller for the hero. Tap one and the hero does two things at once: its header re-writes (title + line) and the flower re-blooms — same species, a different bloom stage.

“Same flower, different bloom” — the options

A · Bloom stages (bud → opening → full)
The same flower shown further open or more closed. Reads as a living thing responding to you. Meaningful: planning = a bud (promise), tonight = full bloom (arrival).
B · Same bloom, tint / mood shift
Only the colourway changes (sage → gold → crimson → plum). Subtle, but on its own it can feel like a filter swap, not a life.
C · Different companion / omen
The flower is constant; the creature on it changes (bee, butterfly, ladybird, dragonfly). Charming, but easy to miss at a glance.
Recommended — A, layered with B + C
Lead with bloom stage (openness) so the flower visibly opens or furls as you tap — that’s the “wow”. Then tint it to the card’s mood and give it a matching companion. Stage carries the meaning; tint + companion make each stop feel like its own little world. Not random — it’s the day of a plate: set it (bud), capture it (opening), come home to it (full bloom).

The transition

The flower morphs — the petal head scales open/closed over ~0.55s with a soft spring, so it genuinely furls and unfurls rather than cutting. Colour and companion cross to the new mood; the title + line swap in step. One tap, one gentle bloom.

Where the actions live now

No promote card means no buried CTA. The selected card’s primary action is a full-width button right under the hero, and it changes with your choice — so “Log tonight’s dinner” is one tap away whenever Tonight is selected, Plan gives “Open the planner”, Snap gives “Snap a photo”, and so on. Everything the old promote card did is still here, just tied to the bloom.

Uniform cards (bible rule)

The five controller cards are identical size (fixed 72×64 tiles, icon + label), in a centred row — matching the new “sliding-row cards must be uniform” rule.
